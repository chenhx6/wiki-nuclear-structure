# 手征振动（Chiral Vibration）

## Definition

静态手征形成前，体系可在左右手构型之间表现为软振动。[[meng-2010-open-problems-nuclear-chirality]] 将其作为 symmetry breaking 的 precursor，并强调当时 TAC+RPA 只覆盖近平衡点的小振幅谐振动。

## Necessary Assumptions

需要存在连接左右手构型的软集体坐标，并明确所用模型如何处理振动、隧穿和角动量投影。

## Discriminating Observables

候选双带劈裂、电磁跃迁 staggering 和角动量几何随自旋的演化可参与判断，但均依赖模型与组态。

## Supporting Evidence

[[meng-2010-open-problems-nuclear-chirality]] 给出理论综述背景；具体核素仍需原始实验与模型来源。

[[luo-2009-chirality-evolution-ru]] provides an original even-even candidate example: `110,112Ru` composite sequences have similar branching-derived ratios and `S(I)`, while planar TAC plus an RPA orientation phonon motivates zero-/one-phonon language. This is source-specific model support, not a universal fingerprint.

[[musangu-2021-chiral-vibrations-104mo-106mo]] adds `104,106Mo` even-even candidates in an extended TPSM framework. Small nearly constant pair splittings and similar rotational response are experimental/derived inputs, whereas the common quasineutron structure, left/right combinations and strongly collective connecting E2 transitions are model results. The predicted connecting transitions are not clearly observed and several `106Mo` expectations sit above experimental upper limits.

## Counter-evidence and Competing Interpretations

普通 `gamma` vibration、带混合或不同组态伙伴带也可能产生双带与相似能量演化。

## Boundary

手征振动与 [[static-chirality]] 是动力学区域而非仅凭双带能量差即可唯一贴上的标签；需要模型角动量几何和电磁跃迁随自旋演化共同约束。

Luo 2009 sharpens the computational boundary: a planar TAC minimum (`φ=0°`) may support a soft orientation mode without static chirality, harmonic RPA covers only small amplitudes, and a band crossing can enter an instability region where direct RPA comparison is invalid. A large-amplitude treatment is then required.

Musangu 2021 sharpens a complementary TPSM boundary: nonzero calculated components do not become direct geometry evidence, especially when shown only for one nucleus and after a `γ=20°→100°` intrinsic-axis reorder. A predicted collective interband E2 network should be tested experimentally before it is used as evidence for reorientation.

Peng and Chen 2022 treats `102Rh` as a planar mean-field case through `0.75 MeV`: this excludes a calculated static aplanar solution in the observed range but does not exclude oscillation of `J` out of the principal plane. Because the calculation contains neither vibration nor tunnelling, that surviving possibility is a boundary statement rather than a predicted side band.

## Our Current Position

当前将其作为 model-dependent dynamical interpretation，不写成仅由能谱直接观测的事实。
