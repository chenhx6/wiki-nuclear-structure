# `136Pm` Yrast/New Chiral-Twin Candidate Pair

## Stable Identity

The pair is the known positive-parity yrast `ΔI=1` band and the new `ΔI=1` band drawn to its left in Hecht 2001 Fig. 1. This source-based label is stable even if later work revises the chiral interpretation or tentative spins.

Starosta 2001 Fig. 2 presents the related yrast/sideband systematics and supplies the common `I=9ℏ` bandhead used by Hecht; the overlapping authors/data lineage means the papers are complementary rather than independent replication.

## Level and Transition Data

The new band contains mixed `M1/E2` `ΔI=1` transitions and `ΔI=2` E2 crossovers, with mixed-dipole and E2 links to the yrast structure. Fig. 1 is the locator for the complete partial scheme; selected 364-keV in-band and 595/684-keV interband transitions receive polarization checks.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Relative spins and multipolarities come from DCO; the electromagnetic character of selected transitions is consistent with M1 from YRAST Ball asymmetry. Bandhead spins are imported from Starosta 2001 systematics and several Fig. 1 labels are parenthesized.

## Configuration Assignment

Hecht 2001 assigns both bands `πh11/2⊗νh11/2` because transition energies, alignments and in-band `B(M1)/B(E2)` ratios are similar. This is an author interpretation, not a direct configuration measurement.

## Proposed Interpretation

Chiral-twin candidate with an approximately `300 keV` splitting attributed to tunnelling between left- and right-handed configurations. The paper's Summary stops at “good candidates.”

Starosta 2001 describes the roughly `0.29 MeV` displacement as a soft collective chiral vibration surrounding the more stable `134Pr` center. This vibrational mode is an author interpretation, not a calculated eigenstate.

## Discriminating Observables

- DCO and selected clover polarization asymmetry；
- interband links and common decay pattern；
- alignment and in-band `B(M1)/B(E2)` comparison；
- 3D TAC aplanar tilt angles and their high-frequency collapse。

## Competing Interpretations

Unfavoured proton signature, quasiparticle excitation and γ vibration are discussed through regional energy systematics, but not all are tested in a common quantitative model. Absolute transition strengths, measured mixing ratios and configuration-sensitive observables remain missing.

The underlying Yale reaction is printed as charge-inconsistent `4n` in Starosta 2001 and `p3n` in Hecht 2001; exact event-set/detector overlap remains unresolved.
