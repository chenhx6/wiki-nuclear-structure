# `104Ag` Bands D and E Electric-Quadrupole Rotational Structures

## Stable Identity

Positive-parity Bands D/E in [[104ag]] are `ΔI=2`-dominated sequences with strong connecting transitions and extensions established in Wang 2013.

## Level and Transition Data

ADO ratios support multiple stretched-quadrupole intraband and dipole linking transitions. Many D/E spins remain tentative, particularly at high excitation.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Positive parity and spins combine previous anchors, ADO multipolarities and Ag-isotope systematics; several assignments are parenthesized.

## Configuration Assignment

D/E are proposed as `πg9/2^-1⊗νd5/2` and `πg9/2^-1⊗νg7/2`, respectively. The assignment transfers `106Ag` calculations/systematics rather than using a new `104Ag` model.

## Proposed Interpretation

They are electric-quadrupole rotational bands whose staggering and strong links may reflect considerable configuration mixing.

## Discriminating Observables

Lifetimes/`Q_t`, mixing ratios, g factors and a `104Ag` configuration-mixing calculation.

## Competing Interpretations

Because the two low-j neutron orbitals mix, one-to-one configuration labels may be oversimplified; systematics alone do not measure intrinsic shapes.
