# `136Nd` D4/D4-C Candidate

## Stable Identity

Negative-parity D4/D4-C is one of Petrache 2018's four weak candidate pairs.

## Level and Transition Data

Relative populations are about `2.85%/0.6%`. D4 shows a backbend and abrupt experimental ratio rise; no partner-resolved ratio comparison is available.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Assignments use DCO, `R_ac`, angular distributions and polarization, with detailed results deferred.

## Configuration Assignment

Configuration D (`β=0.22, γ=19°`) is assigned. A possible six-quasiparticle crossing is invoked but omitted from the fixed-configuration TAC calculation.

## Proposed Interpretation

D4/D4-C remains a weak chiral-doublet candidate contributing to the five-pair MχD census.

## Discriminating Observables

Partner-resolved strengths and crossing/configuration-mixing calculations are required.

## Competing Interpretations

The unmodelled backbend and missing weak-partner ratios allow ordinary configuration crossing/mixing explanations.
