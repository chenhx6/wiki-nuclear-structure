# `138Eu` Yrast/New Chiral-Twin Candidate Pair

## Stable Identity

The pair is the known positive-parity yrast `ΔI=1` band and the new `ΔI=1` band drawn to its left in Hecht 2001 Fig. 1. The identity follows the source's level-scheme relation and does not make the chiral interpretation permanent.

## Level and Transition Data

The new sequence contains `ΔI=1 M1/E2` transitions and `ΔI=2 E2` crossovers, with both multipolarities linking it to the yrast band. Fig. 2(a) shows the sequence in a double-gated spectrum using the 164/299/351/383-keV transitions.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

DCO constrains relative spins and multipolarity, while bandhead spins follow the Starosta 2001 isotone systematics. No `138Eu` linear-polarization result is reported in this paper; several spin labels in Fig. 1 remain parenthesized.

## Configuration Assignment

Both bands are assigned `πh11/2⊗νh11/2` from their similar transition energies, alignments and in-band `B(M1)/B(E2)` ratios. The assignment is inferential and lacks an independent configuration-sensitive measurement.

## Proposed Interpretation

Chiral-twin candidate with an approximately `300 keV` partner displacement. 3D TAC supports an aplanar interval but cannot calculate the quantum mixing/splitting of the observed pair.

## Discriminating Observables

- DCO and the linked partial level scheme；
- double-gated in-band spectrum；
- alignment and `B(M1)/B(E2)` comparison；
- TRS-fed 3D TAC tilt angles and high-frequency return to principal-axis rotation。

## Competing Interpretations

The paper compares against regional unfavoured-signature, quasiparticle and γ-vibrational energy scales, not a full alternative-model calculation. Polarization, lifetimes, absolute strengths and measured mixing ratios remain evidence gaps.
