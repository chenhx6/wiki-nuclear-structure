# `104Ag` Band F Magnetic-Rotation Structure

## Stable Identity

Band F is the high-spin positive-parity `ΔI=1` sequence in [[104ag]] extending to `(20+)` near `7.16 MeV`.

## Level and Transition Data

Wang 2013 extends/presents the sequence in Fig.1 and Table I but does not add lifetime or TAC calculations for it.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Dipole-like in-band behavior and earlier assignments define the magnetic-band identity; the top sequence labels depend on prior work/systematics.

## Configuration Assignment

The paper proposes `πg9/2^-1⊗ν(g7/2/d5/2)(h11/2)^2` by comparison with four-quasiparticle bands in `106,108Ag`.

## Proposed Interpretation

Magnetic rotation is inherited from an earlier TAC-based assignment, not independently demonstrated here through decreasing `B(M1)` or closing-shears observables.

## Discriminating Observables

Lifetimes, absolute `B(M1)` evolution, quadrupole collectivity and a configuration-specific TAC calculation.

## Competing Interpretations

A generic dipole sequence or different four-quasiparticle configuration can resemble a magnetic band without absolute strengths.
