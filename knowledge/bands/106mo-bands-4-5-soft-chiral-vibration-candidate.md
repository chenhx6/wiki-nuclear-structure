# `106Mo` Bands 4 and 5 Soft Chiral-Vibration Candidate

## Stable Identity

Bands 4 and 5 in [[106mo]] are the negative-parity sequences proposed as chiral-vibrational partners in the earlier `106Mo` work and reanalyzed/extended by Musangu 2021.

## Level and Transition Data

Band 4 extends from `5-` at `1952.0 keV` to tentative `(14-)` at `4751.2 keV`. Band 5 contains the new `(3-)` level at `1817.0 keV` and extends through `(12-)` at `4092.1 keV`. Same-spin separations are roughly `100-140 keV`, with similar rotational response over the common range.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

The `517.4-724.3-keV` angular correlation supports the Band-4 `5-` level. The `190.2-517.4` and `226.3-484.6-keV` cascades constrain mixed `6-→5-` and `7-→6-` transitions, but retain discrete mixing-ratio solutions/assumptions. Other assignments combine decay patterns with earlier correlations.

## Configuration Assignment

TPSM assigns the same related `νh11/2` plus mixed `νd5/2-νg7/2` two-quasineutron sector to both sequences. No configuration-sensitive observable independently establishes this mapping.

## Proposed Interpretation

Finite pair splitting, similar rotational response and TPSM energies/ratios motivate soft chiral vibration. Unlike `104Mo`, the paper does not show squared angular-momentum components for `106Mo`.

## Discriminating Observables

Absolute partner strengths and the predicted collective connecting E2 transitions. Table IV gives experimental upper limits below predictions at several spins, making this a direct model test rather than supporting observation.

## Competing Interpretations

Different quasineutron structures, shape fluctuations or ordinary band mixing remain possible. Fixed-deformation TPSM increasingly overestimates high-spin energies and its low-spin inertia staggering differs from experiment.
