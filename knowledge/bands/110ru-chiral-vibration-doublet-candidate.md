# `110Ru` Soft Chiral-Vibration Doublet Candidate

## Stable Identity

The two composite sequences in [[110ru]] are Bands 4-5 and Bands 6-7. Luo 2009 maps them to zero- and one-phonon states, respectively.

## Level and Transition Data

Same-spin energies approach closely; `S(I)` is similar/constant and Table-1 branching-derived `B(E2)/B(M1)` ratios are comparable across the sequences.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Selected level spins and dipole transitions are supported by γ-γ angular correlations. The negative-parity scheme is displayed in Fig.1; precision labels should be checked there.

## Configuration Assignment

The proposed common two-quasineutron configuration excites a neutron from `h11/2` to mixed `d5/2-g7/2`. A tested alternative has similar energy/inertia but incompatible model ratios.

## Proposed Interpretation

Bands 4-5 are a planar TAC zero-phonon reference and Bands 6-7 an RPA orientation phonon near `300 keV`. This is soft chiral vibration, not static chirality because TAC retains `φ=0°`.

## Discriminating Observables

Lifetime-separated strengths for both sequences, mixing ratios, weak interband matrix elements and large-amplitude orientation dynamics.

## Competing Interpretations

Pure-M1/branching assumptions can bias ratio similarity; a different configuration or general orientation vibration may mimic the candidate pattern.
