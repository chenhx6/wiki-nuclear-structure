# `103Rh` Positive-Parity Chiral-Doublet Candidate

## Stable Identity

Kuti 2014 labels the earlier positive-parity pair as Bands 1/2. Suzuki 2008 compares a yrast/partner pair and calls its lifetime-measured member Band 3. Kuti does not explicitly crosswalk that earlier numbering to Band 1 or Band 2; the lifetime values therefore remain attached to the Suzuki member identity, not silently reassigned.

## Level and Transition Data

Suzuki cites separation below `400 keV` and relatively constant `S(I)`; its candidate lifetimes cover `23/2+–29/2+` and strengths cover `25/2+–29/2+`. Kuti reports Bands 1/2 separated by about `500 keV` at `29/2`, approaching about `360 keV` at `39/2`, with similar experimental `B(M1)/B(E2)` ratios.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

The `23/2+` bandhead and positive-parity sequence in [[103rh]] are imported from earlier spectroscopy. Suzuki 2008 does not repeat an angular-distribution or polarization determination, and Kuti 2014 also treats Bands 1/2 as previously identified; absolute spin/parity remains a prior-spectroscopy dependency.

## Configuration Assignment

The proposed configuration is `πg9/2^-1⊗ν(h11/2)^2` with bandhead `23/2+`. It is inherited from earlier spectroscopy; Kuti adds constrained CDFT/TAC-CDFT and PRM support with model-dependent deformation and coupling inputs.

## Proposed Interpretation

Energy and `S(I)` fingerprints plus the one-member lifetime baseline keep the pair as a chiral candidate. Kuti's PRM interprets it as chiral vibration evolving toward near-static chirality and back to another vibration, but this evolution is inherited from a prior model study and does not establish identical partner-band absolute strengths.

## Discriminating Observables

Absolute in-band/interband strengths for both partners, transition mixing ratios and their evolution through the best energy-degeneracy interval.

## Competing Interpretations

Decreasing `B(M1)` may reflect angular-momentum recoupling or shape change. Kuti attributes the too-rapid calculated ratio decrease to a frozen rotor. Without partner lifetimes and an explicit Suzuki/Kuti number crosswalk, ordinary band-specific collectivity and source-identity ambiguity remain.
