# `130Cs` Chiral-Vibration Doublet Candidate

## Stable Identity

The Starosta 2001 pair consists of the known right-hand yrast `ΔI=1` band and the newly identified left-hand sideband in Fig. 2.

## Level and Transition Data

The two positive-parity sequences are connected by reported `ΔI=1` mixed `M1/E2` links. Their spacing patterns are similar, while the sideband remains about `0.25 MeV` higher.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Timing-resolved angular correlations provide relative spins and mixed-link character. The common absolute bandhead `I=9ℏ` is selected from isotone systematics.

## Configuration Assignment

Both bands are assigned `πh11/2⊗νh11/2` from link selection rules and systematics; this is not a direct configuration measurement.

## Proposed Interpretation

Soft collective chiral vibration surrounding the more stable `134Pr` chiral-rotation center.

## Discriminating Observables

Mixed interband links, band displacement, interleaved ΔI=1 spacings and Table I TAC barrier/transition strengths.

## Competing Interpretations

Quasiparticle and γ-vibrational energy scales are discussed, but lifetimes, absolute strengths and a quantum chiral-vibration calculation are absent.
