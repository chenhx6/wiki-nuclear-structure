# `74As` Band 3 Octupole-Correlated Negative-Parity Band

## Stable Identity

Xiao 2022 negative-parity Band 3, experimentally connected to positive-parity Band 1 by three new E1 transitions.

## Level and Transition Data

The high-spin sequence reaches `14−` at `4470.3 keV`. The `774.9`, `919.4` and `1293.4-keV` links connect Band 3 to Band 1. A separate `372.4-keV` E1 joins a lower `7−` level to the positive-parity structure and is not one of the three Band-3 links.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

The scheme uses coincidence relations, ADO and polarization. The three Band-3 links do not each carry individual ADO/polarization values in Table I; their E1 classification also depends on the connected level parities.

## Configuration Assignment

Tentative `π(f5/2/p3/2)⊗νg9/2`. The unresolved proton orbit is motivated by the `g9/2↔p3/2` octupole-coupling region near `Z=34`.

## Proposed Interpretation

Relative `B(E1)/B(E2)` and `δE` resemble `78Br` octupole correlations and differ strongly from stable-octupole `224Th`, supporting dynamic correlations between Bands 1/3.

## Discriminating Observables

Absolute E1 strengths/lifetimes, alternating-parity systematics and a reflection-asymmetric model with configuration mixing.

## Competing Interpretations

Ordinary configuration-dependent E1 decay remains possible without absolute collectivity. The comparison explicitly does not support stable octupole deformation, and `f5/2/p3/2` mixing is unresolved.
