# `136Nd` D2/D2-C Candidate

## Stable Identity

Linked positive-parity D2/D2-C is established by high-fold coincidences, including the sequential-gate D2-C spectra in Figure 2.

## Level and Transition Data

Relative populations are about `1.88%/0.15%`. D2 backbends and its ratio rises abruptly near the configuration change; partner-resolved ratios are unavailable.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Assignments use DCO, `R_ac`, angular distributions and polarization, with details deferred.

## Configuration Assignment

Configuration B (`β=0.22, γ=19°`) is assigned by constrained/TAC-CDFT. The backbend is attributed to a possible eight-quasiparticle crossing outside the fixed-configuration calculation.

## Proposed Interpretation

The source retains D2/D2-C as one of four weaker chiral-doublet candidates contributing to possible MχD.

## Discriminating Observables

Weak-partner lifetimes/ratios and a crossing-aware configuration-mixing calculation.

## Competing Interpretations

A band crossing or configuration mixing can create energy/alignment similarity without a stable chiral pair; absolute D2 ratios are underpredicted.
