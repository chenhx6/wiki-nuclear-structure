# `104Mo` Bands 4 and 5 Soft Chiral-Vibration Candidate

## Stable Identity

Bands 4 and 5 in [[104mo]] are the negative-parity sequences proposed by Musangu 2021 as a chiral-vibrational doublet. Band 4 is the main band and Band 5 the partner in the TPSM analysis.

## Level and Transition Data

Band 4 runs from `4-` at `2060.6 keV` through `(9-)` at `3145.0 keV`. Band 5 runs tentatively from `(5-)` at `2276.5 keV` through `(8-)` at `2932.1 keV`. Common-spin separations remain near `60 keV`. In-band `ΔI=1` and `ΔI=2` transitions and many links to positive-parity structures are tabulated; connecting Band-4/Band-5 transitions are not clearly identified.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Angular correlations for the `477.4-771.1-keV` and `387.8-796.1-keV` cascades support the Band-4 `4-` and `5-` assignments. The Band-5 bandhead is only tentatively `5-`, based on decay pattern and level spacing.

## Configuration Assignment

TPSM attributes both bands to related combinations of one `νh11/2` quasiparticle and one mixed `νd5/2-νg7/2` quasiparticle. This common configuration is calculated rather than experimentally measured.

## Proposed Interpretation

Small nearly constant splitting, similar rotational response and TPSM wavefunctions motivate a soft chiral-vibration interpretation. Nonzero, similar squared angular-momentum components are calculated for both bands, but only after a `γ=20°→100°` intrinsic-axis reordering workaround.

## Discriminating Observables

Partner-resolved lifetimes, absolute in-band and connecting E2/M1 strengths, experimental `I→I-1` interband E2 links and a geometry calculation without the axis-order workaround.

## Competing Interpretations

Different configurations, configuration mixing or a nonchiral collective vibration can reproduce close bands. TPSM predicts large interband E2 collectivity, but the corresponding links are not observed.
