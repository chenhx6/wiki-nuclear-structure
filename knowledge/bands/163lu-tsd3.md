# `163Lu` TSD3

## Stable Identity

Third triaxial strongly deformed sequence in [[jensen-2002-second-phonon-wobbling-163lu]].

## Experimental Relations

- four `ΔI=1` links to TSD2 and eight `ΔI=2` links to TSD1;
- spin/parity fixed through level placement and angular-correlation/distribution evidence;
- TSD2/TSD3 rotational response is nearly identical;
- only the 476-keV TSD3→TSD2 link provides a usable `δ`/relative-strength constraint.

## Level and Transition Data

Four `ΔI=1` links connect TSD3 to TSD2 and eight `ΔI=2` links connect TSD3 to TSD1; the sequence reaches `85/2`.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Level placement plus angular-distribution/DCO results establish the adopted positive-parity sequence, subject to review of JE02-1/2.

## Configuration Assignment

The particle-rotor calculation associates TSD3 with the second favoured member of an aligned `πi13/2` wobbling family. This is a model assignment.

## Proposed Interpretation

The authors assign TSD3 as `n_w=2` wobbling. This depends on the large-E2 mixing-ratio branch, which could not be selected by polarization.

## Discriminating Follow-up

Measure polarization and lifetimes for several TSD3→TSD2 links to test the branch and two-to-one strength scaling independently.

## Discriminating Observables

- polarization and mixing ratios for multiple TSD3→TSD2 links;
- absolute/relative adjacent-band E2 strengths;
- phonon-number scaling and weak direct TSD3→TSD1 decay.

## Competing Interpretations

The small-`abs(δ)` branch would imply an M1-dominated link and a different intrinsic structure; it was rejected by model/systematics rather than polarization.
