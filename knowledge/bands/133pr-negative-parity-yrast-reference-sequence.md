# `133Pr` Negative-Parity Yrast Reference Sequence

## Stable Identity

Singh 2016 uses the known decoupled `πh11/2` yrast band and measures the `310,552,709-keV` decay curves plus limits for the `813/864-keV` transitions.

## Level and Transition Data

Table 2 gives finite lifetimes and `Q_t` at `15/2−,19/2−,23/2−` and limits at `27/2−,31/2−`. The low-spin fit requires roughly `1 ns` cascade side feeding; the `23/2+` feeding level is measured separately and used as a constraint.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

The band scheme and spin/parity labels are imported from earlier spectroscopy. Singh 2016 is a lifetime experiment and does not independently rederive every assignment.

## Configuration Assignment

The decoupled low-Ω `πh11/2` proton assignment is inherited from prior spectroscopy and supported through CHFB/TRS shape-driving systematics.

## Proposed Interpretation

The authors interpret `Q_t≈3.6 eb` with at most marginal decline and TRS minima near `γ=0°` as low-Ω proton stabilization of a near-prolate shape on a γ-soft surface. The inference is limited to below the band crossing.

## Discriminating Observables

Coincidence-gated pre/post-crossing lifetimes, mixing ratios and configuration-sensitive observables would test whether the shape-polarization picture survives the crossing.

## Competing Interpretations

Nearly constant collectivity does not uniquely determine axiality. Long side feeding, a flat γ surface and orbital mixing permit alternative core-response interpretations; the sequence has no measured chiral partner relation.
