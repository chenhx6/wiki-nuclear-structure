# `105Ag` Band E Three-Quasiparticle Reference

## Stable Identity

Timár 2007 Band E is the corrected positive-parity dipole/crossover structure placed through newly observed links to Bands A/C. Its spins are raised by `2ℏ` relative to the earlier scheme.

## Level and Transition Data

The sequence extends approximately from `23/2+` to `41/2+` in Fig.2. The 303-keV transition feeds the `25/2+` level, whose E1/E2 links to A/C fix positive parity and revised placement.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

DCO-constrained 982-keV dipole and 1034-keV quadrupole decay from the `25/2` state favor E1/E2 over implausible M1/M2 intensity ordering, establishing positive parity; repeated dipole/crossover logic propagates the assignments.

## Configuration Assignment

Experimental Routhians, alignments and branching-derived ratios agree with TRS/geometrical-model expectations for `πg9/2⊗ν(h11/2)^2`.

## Proposed Interpretation

This was the intended chiral target. No side band was observed above about 0.1 of Band E's population, so the source reports no chiral partner and infers a more axial/γ-rigid shape.

## Discriminating Observables

Corrected links/spins, DCO, Routhian/alignment, `B(M1)/B(E2)` and the explicit side-band population threshold.

## Competing Interpretations

A partner below sensitivity is not excluded. The axial/γ-rigid shape follows TRS/TAC comparison rather than a direct shape observable.
