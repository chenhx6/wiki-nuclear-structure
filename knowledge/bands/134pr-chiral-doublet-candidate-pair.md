# `134Pr` Band 1/Band 2 Chiral-Doublet Candidate Pair

## Stable Identity

This page crosswalks Petrache 1996 Bands 1 and 2 to the right-hand yrast band and left-hand sideband in Starosta 2001 Fig. 2. The mapping is retrospective: Petrache 1996 did not claim chirality and left Band 2 alternatives unresolved.

## Level and Transition Data

Petrache 1996 provides the underlying links, DCO, alignment and branching-derived strength systematics. Starosta 2001 reports an energy displacement decreasing from about `0.19 MeV` at low spin to near degeneracy at `I=14`.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Positive-parity relative structure follows the earlier level scheme and mixed links. Starosta uses a common `I=9ℏ` bandhead systematics across the four isotones.

## Configuration Assignment

Starosta interprets both bands as `πh11/2⊗νh11/2`; Petrache 1996 had rejected a simple unfavoured-signature picture for Band 2 but could not resolve shape/γ-phonon/mixing alternatives. The later common-configuration assignment therefore remains interpretive.

## Proposed Interpretation

Starosta 2001 treats this as the most nearly degenerate, central chiral-restored doublet of the `N=75` island.

## Discriminating Observables

Band links, DCO, energy displacement versus spin, alignment/`B(M1)/B(E2)`, and TAC deformation/barrier/transition-strength systematics.

## Competing Interpretations

Petrache's shape coexistence, γ-phonon coupling and configuration-mixing/model-deficiency alternatives remain historical counter-interpretations. Starosta disfavours conventional qp/γ-vibrational energies but does not calculate all alternatives against the same observables.
