# `104Rh` Negative-Parity Chiral-Doublet Candidate

## Stable Identity

The pair is the `104Rh` yrast/partner structure in Suzuki 2008 Fig.1. The paper reports five lifetimes for one sequence and explicitly leaves the partner-band lifetimes for future work.

## Level and Transition Data

The measured member covers `9−–13−`. `B(M1)` declines monotonically, while `B(E2)` alternates weakly and causes the earlier `B(M1)/B(E2)` staggering.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

The `8−` bandhead and negative-parity sequence in [[104rh]] are imported from the earlier level scheme. Suzuki 2008 measures lifetimes and does not independently repeat the spin/parity assignment.

## Configuration Assignment

The assigned `πg9/2^-1⊗νh11/2` bandhead is `8−`. Perpendicular valence coupling is a semiclassical author interpretation, not a measured angular-momentum geometry.

## Proposed Interpretation

The energy/`S(I)` pattern and lifetime-derived strengths support retaining a candidate. Partner equality, high-spin convergence and a quantum chiral wave function remain unestablished.

## Discriminating Observables

Partner-band and higher-spin lifetimes, mixing ratios, absolute interband strengths and a dedicated calculation of both sequences.

## Competing Interpretations

The `B(E2)` staggering has no source-level explanation. Shape evolution and single-particle/shape coupling can compete with the chiral-coupling narrative.
