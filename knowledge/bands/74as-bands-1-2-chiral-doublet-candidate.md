# `74As` Bands 1-2 Chiral-Doublet Candidate

## Stable Identity

Xiao 2022 positive-parity Bands 1/2. Band 1 is the extended yrast sequence; Band 2 is newly established and linked to Band 1 by five transitions.

## Level and Transition Data

Band 1 runs from `8+` at `604.6 keV` through `(16+)` at `5659.6 keV`. Band 2 runs from `9+` at `1421.5 keV` through `(14+)` at `4373.0 keV`. Four `ΔI=1 M1/E2` and one `ΔI=2 E2` links connect the sequences; the pair remains about `400 keV` apart.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Coincidences, ADO and polarization constrain the scheme. Band-1 spins are `1ℏ` higher than Ref.32; `70,72,74As` yrast systematics support the revision. An `≈11.8-keV` gap below the low-lying `55-keV` transition remains unresolved.

## Configuration Assignment

The proposed common configuration is `πg9/2⊗νg9/2`, based on the earlier Band-1 assignment and the five-link network. It is an author assignment, not directly measured occupation.

## Proposed Interpretation

Similar `S(I)`, close staggered `B(M1)/B(E2)` and a fitted TPRM support a chiral-doublet interpretation. TPRM uses `β2=0.37`, fitted `γ=21.6°` and `J=12ℏ²/MeV`.

## Discriminating Observables

Partner-resolved absolute strengths, configuration-sensitive observables and a model that predicts energy/transition data without tuning `γ/J` to the same pair.

## Competing Interpretations

The `≈400-keV` separation is not strict degeneracy. The calculated neutron angular momentum is mixed among all three axes rather than aligned along the long axis; only total `I` is aplanar. No lifetimes test absolute partner similarity.
