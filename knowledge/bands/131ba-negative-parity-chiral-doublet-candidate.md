# `131Ba` D7/D8 Negative-Parity Pair

## Stable Identity

D7/D8 are a linked negative-parity pair in Guo 2020. D8 is new and D7 is extended. The 715/721-keV M1/E2 links support common parity and relative placement.

## Level and Transition Data

Figure 1 gives the D7/D8 sequences and links. D7 also sends many E1 transitions to D3-D6; more than half of its observed intensity follows this opposite-parity decay path.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

The D8 spin/parity placement uses its observed ΔI=1 and ΔI=2 links to D7 plus `R_ac` systematics. Some highest-spin labels remain tentative in the partial scheme.

## Configuration Assignment

RAT-PRM assigns dominant `π(h11/2)^2⊗νh11/2` components to D7/D8. This is supported by alignment and neighboring-nucleus systematics but is not a direct orbital measurement.

## Proposed Interpretation

Energy, `S(I)`, branching-derived ratios and alignment similarities to `133Ce/135Nd`, together with RAT-PRM, motivate a `π(h11/2)^2⊗νh11/2` chiral-doublet interpretation.

## Octupole-Correlation Link

More than half of D7 intensity feeds D3-D6 through many E1 transitions. These direct opposite-parity links support octupole correlations between the proposed chiral systems without an intermediary nonchiral configuration; they do not establish stable octupole deformation.

## Discriminating Observables

Absolute strengths and mixing ratios, D8-to-quartet E1 mapping, and the deferred pair-specific angular-momentum geometry.

## Competing Interpretations

The pair assignment is built from comparative fingerprints and a model configuration; neither lifetime equality nor the claimed chiral geometry is shown in this paper.
