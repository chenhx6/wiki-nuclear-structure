# `108Ru` Negative-Parity Doublet Structures

## Stable Identity

Luo 2009's Bands 4-5 and 6-7 form two composite negative-parity `ΔI=1` sequences in [[108ru]]. They are retained as structural identities, not as established chiral partners.

## Level and Transition Data

Figure 1 gives levels/links. Bands 4-5 develop strong even-odd energy staggering, whereas Bands 6-7 form a smoother `ΔI=1` sequence.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Angle-sorted correlations support selected spins and dipole depopulating transitions. Negative parity is an experimental assignment, and several detailed labels still require Fig.1 review.

## Configuration Assignment

No stable common configuration is established. Different quasiparticle structures remain possible.

## Proposed Interpretation

The authors prefer γ softness disturbing a would-be doublet by analogy with γ-soft `106Ag`, but explicitly stop further discussion.

## Discriminating Observables

Absolute strengths, mixing ratios, configuration-sensitive moments and a γ-soft large-amplitude calculation.

## Competing Interpretations

The unlike signature behavior may simply mark different quasiparticle configurations rather than chiral dynamics.
