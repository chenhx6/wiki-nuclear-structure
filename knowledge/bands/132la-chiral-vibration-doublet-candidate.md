# `132La` Chiral-Vibration Doublet Candidate

## Stable Identity

The Starosta 2001 pair consists of the known right-hand yrast `ΔI=1` band and newly identified left-hand sideband in Fig. 2.

## Level and Transition Data

Mixed `M1/E2` links connect the positive-parity sequences. The sideband tracks the yrast spacing pattern but remains about `0.37 MeV` higher.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Angular-correlation timing constrains relative spins and link character; the common `I=9ℏ` bandhead is systematics-based.

## Configuration Assignment

Both bands are assigned `πh11/2⊗νh11/2` through link selection rules and isotone comparison, not a direct configuration measurement.

## Proposed Interpretation

Soft collective chiral vibration outside the central near-degenerate `134Pr` case.

## Discriminating Observables

Mixed links, band displacement, interleaved spacing pattern and 3D TAC aplanar/barrier systematics.

## Competing Interpretations

Quasiparticle and γ-vibrational energy scales are argued higher; absolute strengths and a quantum vibration calculation are missing.
