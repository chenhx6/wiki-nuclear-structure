# `104Ag` Bands A and B Chiral-Doublet Candidate

## Stable Identity

Bands A/B are the linked negative-parity sequences in [[104ag]] Fig.1. B's new proposed bandhead is `(10−)` at `2212 keV`; many B labels remain tentative.

## Level and Transition Data

Strong M1 links connect the sequences. Same-spin separation decreases with spin to below `300 keV` at `I=15`, but does not reach `104Rh`-like degeneracy.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Coincidence relations and ADO values constrain dipole/quadrupole transitions and spin changes. Several labels are parenthesized, and no linear polarization is reported.

## Configuration Assignment

Band A inherits `πg9/2^-1⊗νh11/2` from earlier axial-rotor-plus-two-quasiparticle work; Band B receives the same assignment from neighboring-systematics.

## Proposed Interpretation

Smooth/similar `S(I)`, in-phase ratio staggering and nearly identical alignments motivate chirality. The source locally says “true chiral partners” but concludes candidate; the stable Wiki status follows the conservative conclusion.

## Discriminating Observables

Absolute lifetimes, mixing ratios, interband strengths, polarization and a dedicated γ-soft chiral calculation for both bands.

## Competing Interpretations

Common configuration, γ-soft vibration, signature effects or band mixing can mimic the fingerprints without an aplanar handed geometry.
