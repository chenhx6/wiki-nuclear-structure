# `140Eu` Bands 1 and 2 Doublet Candidate

## Stable Identity

The identity is the linked negative-parity Band 1/Band 2 pair in Hecht 2003 Fig. 1. A nearby five-transition sideband is only coincident with Band 1 and lacks identified links, so it is not silently merged into this stable pair.

## Level and Transition Data

Band 1 feeds the 125-ms `5−` isomer. Band 2 is linked to Band 1 by 821.0- and 709.4-keV E2 transitions and 401.4- and 285.4-keV mixed M1/E2 transitions. Band 2 changes from E2-dominated low-spin decay to dipole-dominated behavior after a crossing near `I≈12ℏ`.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

The 285.4-keV transition feeds the `5−` isomer and anchors negative parity with the lowest observed Band-2 level assigned `I=6`.

## Configuration Assignment

Both bands are assigned mixed `π(g7/2,d5/2)⊗νh11/2`; the authors explicitly caution against a pure `g7/2` or `d5/2` proton label.

## Proposed Interpretation

Same-spin separations of about 110 keV at `6−/8−` and about 10 keV at `10−` motivate a speculative chiral-twin interpretation. The same TRS calculation also permits the pair to occupy distinct `γ≈±25°` minima, so shape coexistence remains coequal.

## Discriminating Observables

Interband E2 and mixed M1/E2 links, same-spin energy separations, different crossing behavior, alignment and branching-derived `B(M1)/B(E2)` provide the current comparison set. Lifetimes and absolute strengths are the proposed decisive additions.

## Competing Interpretations

- Band 1 and Band 2 show different crossing behavior.
- Band 2's signature partner is unobserved.
- Band-1 branching-derived `B(M1)/B(E2)` disagrees strongly with the particle-rotor calculation.
- No lifetimes, absolute strengths or configuration-specific observables are reported.
