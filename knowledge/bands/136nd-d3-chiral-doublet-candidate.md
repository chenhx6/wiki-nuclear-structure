# `136Nd` D3/D3-C Candidate

## Stable Identity

Negative-parity D3/D3-C is one of four weak pairs, but it is the only one used for an explicit 3D TAC-CDFT azimuth-angle calculation.

## Level and Transition Data

Relative populations are about `2.55%/0.21%`. D3 shows a backbend and abrupt experimental ratio rise not reproduced by fixed-configuration TAC; D3-C lacks measured partner ratios.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Assignments use the combined multipolarity/polarization program with deferred details.

## Configuration Assignment

Configuration D* (`β=0.21, γ=22°`) is assigned. A possible six-quasiparticle crossing is invoked at the backbend but lies outside the calculation.

## Proposed Interpretation

For D3, 3D TAC changes from planar `φ=0` to aplanar `φ>0` above `ħω≈0.5 MeV`, strongly supporting chiral rotation of the assigned intrinsic configuration. It does not itself calculate partner tunnelling or D3-C electromagnetic equality.

## Discriminating Observables

D3-C lifetimes/ratios and a crossing-aware quantum calculation of the doublet splitting and transitions.

## Competing Interpretations

Configuration change at backbend and the missing partner ratios prevent the mean-field aplanar solution from uniquely confirming the observed pair.
