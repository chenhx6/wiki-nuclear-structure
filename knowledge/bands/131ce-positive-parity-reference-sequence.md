# `131Ce` Positive-Parity Reference Sequence

## Stable Identity

Li 2004 Fig.1 gives a separate `7/2+` to `25/2+` sequence with 257/610/725/796/649-keV transitions. It is not merged with the negative-parity Band-1-like lineage and is not treated as its chiral partner.

## Level and Transition Data

Table 1 reports finite lifetimes and `Q_t` at `13/2+`, `17/2+` and `21/2+`; `25/2+` has a feeding-uncorrected effective upper lifetime limit. The 725-keV line shape is displayed in Fig.2(b).

## Spin-Parity Evidence

The page inherits Li 2004's partial-scheme assignments; this lifetime paper does not independently reconstruct all spin-parity evidence.

## Configuration Assignment

The author associates the band with neutron `[404]7/2+` from `g7/2`. This regional assignment supplies shape-driving context, not a direct orbital measurement.

## Proposed Interpretation

Average `Q_t=4.56(145) eb` is interpreted as larger than the negative-parity value and compatible with a more prolate shape-driving response.

## Discriminating Observables

The three finite lifetimes, one limit, `B(E2)`/`Q_t` values and the 725-keV fitted line shape provide the source-level evidence.

## Competing Interpretations

The apparent parity contrast has large uncertainties and is sensitive to feeding and rotational extraction. It does not by itself demonstrate stable shape coexistence.
