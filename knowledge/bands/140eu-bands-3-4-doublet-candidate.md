# `140Eu` Bands 3 and 4 Doublet Candidate

## Stable Identity

The identity is the linked positive-parity Band 3/Band 4 pair in Hecht 2003 Fig. 1. Band 4 is a revised placement of a previously reported side-feeding cascade.

## Level and Transition Data

Band 4 is connected to Band 3 by 787.3- and 511.5-keV E2 links and 502.1-, 490.4- and 483.5-keV mixed M1/E2 links. The comparable in-band and interband intensities constrain the level placement. Band 3 reaches `25+`; its spins are revised upward by `2ℏ` from Ref. 17 using regional systematics and a new 71.0-keV `10+→9+` placement.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Link multipolarities establish common positive parity. Band 3 spins are revised upward by `2ℏ` from Ref. 17 through regional systematics and the 71.0-keV placement.

## Configuration Assignment

Selection-rule reasoning, similar initial alignment near `7ℏ` and regional systematics support the author's common `πh11/2⊗νh11/2` assignment. This is a multi-observable inference, not direct measurement of the intrinsic configuration.

## Proposed Interpretation

Same-spin separations are about 30 keV at `13ℏ/15ℏ` and 140 keV at `11ℏ/17ℏ`, making this the stronger of the paper's two chiral-twin candidates. However, the `γ≈±25°` TRS minima allow Band 3 and Band 4 to be the same quasiparticle configuration at different shapes.

## Discriminating Observables

Multiple E2 and mixed M1/E2 links, same-spin energy separations, alignment/backbend behavior and branching-derived `B(M1)/B(E2)` form the comparison set. Absolute `B(E2)` evolution near `I≈15ℏ` is the paper's proposed discriminator.

## Competing Interpretations

- Band 4's signature partner is unobserved.
- Band 4 does not extend far enough to complete the alignment/backbend comparison.
- Branching-derived `B(M1)/B(E2)` for Band 3 agrees only approximately above `I=13ℏ`; individual absolute strengths are absent.
- Lifetimes are explicitly required to discriminate abrupt chiral evolution from smooth shape coexistence.
