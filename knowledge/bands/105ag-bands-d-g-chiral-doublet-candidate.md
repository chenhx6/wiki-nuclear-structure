# `105Ag` Bands D and G Chiral-Doublet Candidate

## Stable Identity

The pair is Timár 2007's linked negative-parity Bands D/G in Fig.2. Band G is new; Band D's known lower section is extended. Several high-spin labels are tentative.

## Level and Transition Data

The two bands are connected by relatively strong transitions and remain about `70 keV` apart over their common-spin region. Figure 6 compares excitation energies, `S(I)` and branching-derived `B(M1)/B(E2)`.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Band D follows the known negative-parity dipole/crossover structure. Band G spins use the stretched-dipole link to Band C and DCO; negative parity is strongly favored by several G↔D links but remains tentative at high spin.

## Configuration Assignment

TRS/Routhian/alignment and ratio comparisons assign related `πg9/2⊗νh11/2(g7/2,d5/2)` natural-parity configurations. The exact `g7/2/d5/2` content/mixing is unresolved.

## Proposed Interpretation

Small same-spin separation, smooth `S(I)` and similar staggered `B(M1)/B(E2)` meet Koike fingerprint criteria. The paper calls D/G a good, nonideal chiral candidate.

## Discriminating Observables

Interband links, ≈70-keV separation, `S(I)`, branching-derived ratio staggering and future absolute in-/out-of-band matrix elements.

## Competing Interpretations

No dedicated TAC/PRM calculation establishes D/G aplanarity. Natural-parity mixing between `g7/2` and `d5/2` configurations can produce the observed similarities; the authors cannot distinguish it from chirality.
