# `136Nd` D5/D5-Chiral Pair

## Stable Identity

D5 and D5-chiral are the strongest of Petrache 2018's five linked pairs and the only pair with experimental `B(M1)/B(E2)` values for both members.

## Level and Transition Data

Figure 1 shows D5 relative population `8.43%` and D5-chiral `1.9%` relative to the `374-keV 2+→0+` line. Their experimental ratios are nearly identical within errors and decrease at the highest spins.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Spin/parity relies on the paper's combined DCO, `R_ac`, angular-distribution and polarization program; its detailed transition-by-transition audit is deferred.

## Configuration Assignment

Configuration C is positive parity with constrained-CDFT `β=0.26, γ=23°` and a six-quasiparticle spherical-orbital content. TAC-CDFT reproduces its ratio trend over the observed range.

## Proposed Interpretation

This is the paper's one “clear chiral character” pair and first firm experimental case claimed in an even-even nucleus. Wiki retains it as the clearest candidate because no pair-specific 3D aplanar calculation or absolute lifetime matrix is shown.

## Discriminating Observables

Absolute partner-resolved matrix elements and a D5-specific 3D dynamical calculation including left/right quantum mixing.

## Competing Interpretations

Common multiquasiparticle rotation or configuration mixing may imitate related ratios. The 3D TAC result shown for D3 cannot be transferred directly to D5.
