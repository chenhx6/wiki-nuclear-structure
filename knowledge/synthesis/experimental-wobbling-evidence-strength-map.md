# Experimental wobbling evidence strength map

## Active Summary for Agents

This synthesis compares 15 original/reanalysis sources ingested on 2026-07-16. It does not treat all reported cases as equally established. Review priority is controlled by link multipolarity/branch selection, lifetime coverage, band/configuration identity and model independence. All `WOB-EXP-*` statements remain `needs_review: true`.

## Question and Scope

How did reported experimental wobbling evidence evolve from high-spin Lu TSD bands to normal-deformed low/medium-spin, multiphonon, two-quasiparticle and chiral-wobbler candidates, and where do the evidence chains remain conditional?

## Evolution and transfer boundaries

1. **High-spin TSD phase:** Lu established the basic link-multipolarity/relative-strength paradigm, but later Lu/Ta cases show that not every isotope had equivalent polarization evidence.
2. **Normal-deformed low-spin phase:** `105Pd/133La/127Xe/133Ba/151Eu` add DCO/polarization and energy-trend arguments, while TiP/IBFM alternatives and approximation limits become more important.
3. **Configuration-generalization phase:** `183Au` introduces multiple parity/configuration families; `136Nd` changes the expected link character for two quasiparticles; `74Br` combines chirality, wobbling, lifetimes and absolute strengths.
4. **Reanalysis phase:** `129Ba` shows that legacy angular data can motivate a possibility but should not be promoted to observation without new direct measurements.

## Counter-evidence

- [[chakraborty-2023-131xe-wobbling-origin]] provides the current direct counterexample in which triaxial-model context coexists with M1-dominated links and a signature-partner interpretation.
- [[lv-2022-evidence-against-wobbling-135pr]] and [[guo-2022-low-spin-wobbling-187au]] show that disputed `δ` branches can reverse low-spin assignments.
- TiP and IBFM sources demonstrate that enhanced links or band-energy reproduction can occur in alternative dynamical descriptions.

## Model Dependence

- High-spin Lu/Ta cases use particle-rotor/UC configuration comparisons.
- Low-spin odd-A cases use CDFT+PRM, QTR, TPSM or IBFM with different deformation, MoI, alignment and electromagnetic inputs.
- `136Nd` fits the E2 operator and `g_eff/Q` to sparse electromagnetic data; `74Br` uses PRM to map three bands onto chiral/wobbling geometry.
- Cross-case agreement does not imply independent model confirmation when authors, inputs or theoretical lineage overlap.

## Limitations and Missing Evidence

- Measure full `χ²(δ)`/likelihood branches with calibrated polarization for every decisive link.
- Add lifetimes/absolute `B(E2)` and `B(M1)` across several adjacent bands, not one normalization point.
- Test wobbling, TiP, signature partner and IBFM/QTR/PRM with common configurations and inputs.
- Separate triaxiality evidence from wobbling-band identity to avoid circular inference.
- Retrieve Guo 2024 Supplemental Material and the original `129Ba` experimental papers before paper-level use.
