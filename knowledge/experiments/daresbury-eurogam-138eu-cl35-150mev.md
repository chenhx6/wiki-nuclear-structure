# Daresbury Eurogam `106Cd(35Cl,2pn)138Eu` Experiment

## Identity

Previously acquired Eurogam Phase I data reanalysed by Hecht 2001 to establish a new `138Eu` `ΔI=1` partner sequence and its links to the known yrast band.

## Beam, Target and Reaction

- `35Cl` beam at 150 MeV from the Daresbury Tandem；
- `106Cd(35Cl,2pn)138Eu` fusion-evaporation channel。

The paper does not specify target thickness or backing in its compact experiment description.

## Detector Configuration

Eurogam Phase I with 45 large-volume Ge detectors.

## Trigger and Coincidence Conditions

About `5.7×10^8` unfolded γ-γ coincidences were recorded. The paper reports doubles/triples analysis but not the original hardware trigger or unfolding implementation.

## Data Products

- partial `138Eu` level scheme and new-band links；
- double-gated spectrum using 164/299/351/383-keV transitions；
- DCO-based relative spin/multipolarity constraints；
- alignment and branching-derived `B(M1)/B(E2)` comparisons。

## Nuclei and Bands Studied

[[138eu]] and [[138eu-chiral-twin-candidate-pair]]。

## Known Limitations

The data set had already been used for earlier `138Eu` high-spin spectroscopy; this paper is a targeted reanalysis. No `138Eu` polarization result, lifetime or absolute transition strength is reported, and several spin assignments rely on external systematics.
