# CIAE HI-13 `116Sn(19F,p3n)131Ce` DSAM Experiment

## Identity

Li 2004 experiment measuring lifetimes in the negative- and positive-parity high-spin sequences of `131Ce` through Doppler-broadened line shapes.

## Beam, Target and Reaction

- 95 MeV `19F` beam from the HI-13 tandem;
- isotopically enriched `116Sn` target, `1.05 mg/cm²`;
- `22.7 mg/cm²` Pb backing used to stop recoils;
- `116Sn(19F,p3n)131Ce` fusion-evaporation channel.

## Detector Configuration

Eleven BGO Compton-suppressed HPGe detectors: three at `90°`, four at `48°` and four at `132°`, each 25 cm from the target. `60Co` and `152Eu` sources supplied relative-efficiency and energy calibration.

## Trigger and Coincidence Conditions

About `10^8` event-by-event γ-γ coincidences were acquired. For DSAM, one γ was required in the `132°` detectors and the other in any remaining detector; gates on relevant `131Ce` transitions suppressed overlapping peaks.

## Data Products

- partial negative-/positive-parity level scheme;
- 827- and 725-keV fitted line shapes;
- level lifetimes or limits, `B(E2)` and `Q_t` in Table 1;
- Ce-isotope `Q_t` systematics.

## Nuclei and Bands Studied

[[131ce]], [[131ce-negative-parity-yrast-reference-sequence]] and [[131ce-positive-parity-reference-sequence]].

## Known Limitations

The line-shape model depends on stopping powers, angular straggling, detector response, cascade feeding and phenomenological side feeding. The `31/2−` and `25/2+` entries are effective upper limits without feeding correction; the `19/2−` entry is a lower limit inferred from absence of visible broadening. Raw spectra and feeding histories are not in the Wiki.
