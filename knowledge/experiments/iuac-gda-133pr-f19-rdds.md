# IUAC GDA `133Pr` RDDS Experiment

## Identity

Plunger experiment measuring the negative-parity `πh11/2` yrast sequence and selected positive-parity feeding states in `133Pr`.

## Beam, Target and Reaction

`92-MeV` `19F` on about `1 mg/cm²`, `92%` enriched `118Sn` rolled on about `3 mg/cm²` Au; the backing faced the beam. The stopper was about `6 mg/cm²` Au, and the minimum calibrated distance was about `7 μm`.

## Detector Configuration

The IUAC GDA used HPGe groups at `50°/98°/144°` with a BGO multiplicity condition. Coincidence data were insufficient, so multiplicity-gated singles from each angular group were gain-matched and summed.

## Analysis Products

LIFETIME fits shifted/unshifted fractions with cascade and three-level band feeding. The measured `23/2+` feeding lifetime constrains the yrast fit; prior level schemes and transition intensities inform side feeding.

## Known Limitations

Singles-only lifetime extraction provides less feeding selectivity than coincidence-gated DDCM. The `15/2−` fit needs about `1 ns` cascade feeding, and modelled band feeding is restricted using an earlier `Q_t=4–5 eb` estimate. Shape conclusions apply below the band crossing.
