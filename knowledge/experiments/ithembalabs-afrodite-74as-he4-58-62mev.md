# iThemba AFRODITE `74As` Experiment

## Identity

Xiao 2022 high-spin γ-spectroscopy dataset for `74As`.

## Beam, Target and Reaction

`58.6/62.6-MeV` `4He` beams on a `2.85 mg/cm²` `74Ge` target with `10.8 mg/cm²` carbon backing; `74Ge(4He,1p3n)74As`.

## Detector Configuration

Eight Compton-suppressed AFRODITE clovers plus two LEPS detectors. Four clovers were at `135°`; four clovers and both LEPS detectors were at `90°`. Energy/efficiency calibration used `152Eu`.

## Trigger and Coincidence Conditions

Approximately `1.9×10^9` γ-γ coincidences were sorted into one symmetric matrix and several asymmetric matrices.

## Data Products

Coincidence spectra and level scheme; ADO ratios; clover polarization asymmetry `A_p`; transition energies/intensities and spin-parity assignments.

## Nuclei and Bands Studied

[[74as]], especially [[74as-bands-1-2-chiral-doublet-candidate]] and [[74as-band-3-octupole-correlated-negative-parity]].

## Known Limitations

No lifetime dataset or absolute transition strengths. The paper refers to Ref.24 for further setup/procedure details, and the low-energy scheme retains an unobserved `≈11.8-keV` gap.
