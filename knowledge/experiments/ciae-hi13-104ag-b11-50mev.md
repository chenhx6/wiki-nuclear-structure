# CIAE HI-13 `97Mo(11B,4n)104Ag` Experiment

## Identity

Wang 2013 γ-γ/ADO experiment used to revise the `104Ag` A-F high-spin scheme.

## Beam, Target and Reaction

- `11B` beam at `50 MeV`;
- `2.0 mg/cm²`, `94.25%` enriched `97Mo` on `8.0 mg/cm²` natural Pb;
- `97Mo(11B,4n)104Ag`.

## Detector Configuration

Thirteen Compton-suppressed HPGe detectors at forward, `90°` and backward angles. Standard `60Co`, `133Ba` and `152Eu` sources calibrated energy/efficiency.

## Trigger and Coincidence Conditions

At least two suppressed detectors within an `80 ns` prompt window. The experiment recorded `1.5×10^8` events and sorted a symmetric γ-γ matrix plus asymmetric all-angle/angle matrices.

## ADO Calibration

`R_ADO=Iγ(40° or 140°)/Iγ(90°)` with all-angle gates. Typical values are `1.2` for stretched quadrupole and `≈0.8` for pure dipole transitions.

## Data Products

- revised A-F scheme with more than 40 new transitions;
- coincidence/intensity/energy-sum placements;
- ADO multipolarities and spin-parity assignments;
- energy, `S(I)`, ratio and alignment comparisons.

## Known Limitations

No polarization or lifetime measurement. Weak transitions give uncertain ADO values and several parenthesized spin/parity assignments. Configuration/chirality conclusions rely on earlier calculations and neighboring systematics.
