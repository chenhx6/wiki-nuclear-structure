# Stony Brook LINAC `124Sn(10B,4n)130Cs` Experiment

## Identity

Starosta 2001 data set used to extend the `130Cs` yrast structure, establish a new sideband and identify mixed interband links.

## Beam, Target and Reaction

- 47 MeV `10B` beam；
- 2-3 mg/cm² `124Sn` target backed with Pb；
- `124Sn(10B,4n)130Cs`。

## Detector Configuration

Suppressed Ge detectors at `±30°`, `±90°` and `±150°` relative to the beam, with a multiplicity filter. The article does not state detector count/efficiency.

## Trigger and Coincidence Conditions

Coincidence data with a quoted `±20 ns` timing window were used for γ angular correlations. The hardware trigger and event count are not reported.

## Data Products

Partial level scheme, yrast extension, new sideband, mixed-link angular-correlation assignments and isotone energy systematics.

## Nuclei and Bands Studied

[[130cs]] and [[130cs-chiral-vibration-doublet-candidate]]。

## Known Limitations

Only relative spins are experimentally constrained in this article; common absolute bandhead and configuration depend on systematics/selection rules. No lifetime or absolute transition strength is reported.
