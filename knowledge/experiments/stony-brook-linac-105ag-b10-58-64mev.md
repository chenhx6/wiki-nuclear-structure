# Stony Brook `100Mo(10B,5n)105Ag` Experiment

## Identity

Timár 2007 high-sensitivity γ-γ/DCO experiment used to reconstruct the A–G level scheme, search for a Band-E partner and identify D/G candidate similarities.

## Beam, Target and Reaction

- `10B` beam at 58 and 64 MeV;
- enriched `100Mo` target `1.3 mg/cm²` on `20 mg/cm²` natural Pb backing;
- `100Mo(10B,5n)105Ag`.

## Detector Configuration

Six Compton-suppressed HPGe detectors and a 14-element BGO multiplicity filter. A `152Eu` target-position source calibrated Ge energy and efficiency.

## Trigger and Coincidence Conditions

Approximately `10^8` γ-γ coincidences were collected. Forward/backward versus `90°` asymmetric matrices supplied DCO ratios.

## Data Products

- A–G partial level scheme and Table-I transitions;
- DCO multipolarities and corrected Band-E links;
- Routhians, alignments, `S(I)` and branching-derived `B(M1)/B(E2)`;
- sensitivity-bounded Band-E side-band null result.

## Nuclei and Bands Studied

[[105ag]], [[105ag-band-e-three-quasiparticle-reference]] and [[105ag-bands-d-g-chiral-doublet-candidate]].

## Known Limitations

Only six Ge detectors were used; weak high-spin lines have large uncertainties and several spin/parity assignments are tentative. The Band-E null applies to side bands populated above about one tenth of Band E. No lifetimes or polarization are measured.
