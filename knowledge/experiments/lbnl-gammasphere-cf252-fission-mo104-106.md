# LBNL Gammasphere `252Cf` Spontaneous-Fission `104,106Mo` Analysis

## Identity

High-statistics prompt-fission coincidence analysis used by Musangu 2021 to revise collective and negative-parity structures in `104,106Mo`.

## Beam, Target and Reaction

A `62 μCi 252Cf` source was sandwiched between two `10 mg/cm²` Fe foils and mounted in a `7.62-cm` plastic ball. No beam-target reaction was used.

## Detector Configuration

101 Gammasphere Ge detectors recorded prompt γ rays at LBNL. Their angular grouping enabled γ-γ angular correlations.

## Trigger and Coincidence Conditions

The data were sorted into `5.7×10^11` triple-and-higher-fold and `1.9×10^11` quadruple-and-higher-fold coincidence events and analyzed with RADWARE.

## Data Products

- revised and extended `104,106Mo` level schemes and large transition tables;
- double-/triple-gated spectra separating weak lines from contaminants and fission partners;
- selected angular-correlation coefficients and mixing-ratio solutions;
- energy-splitting, rotational-response and branching-ratio inputs for TPSM comparison.

## Nuclei and Bands Studied

[[104mo-bands-4-5-soft-chiral-vibration-candidate]] and [[106mo-bands-4-5-soft-chiral-vibration-candidate]], plus ground-state and multiphonon-γ structures summarized on [[104mo]] and [[106mo]].

## Known Limitations

No lifetimes or absolute experimental transition probabilities are measured. Weak proposed interband transitions are absent or only upper-limited. The source strength and triple-event count match [[gammasphere-cf252-fission-ru108-112]], so the two papers are not counted as independent experimental replication without run-level provenance.
