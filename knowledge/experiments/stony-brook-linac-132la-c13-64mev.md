# Stony Brook LINAC `123Sb(13C,4n)132La` Experiment

## Identity

Starosta 2001 data set used to extend the `132La` yrast structure, establish a new sideband and identify mixed interband links.

## Beam, Target and Reaction

- 64 MeV `13C` beam；
- 2-3 mg/cm² `123Sb` target backed with Pb；
- `123Sb(13C,4n)132La`。

## Detector Configuration

Suppressed Ge detectors at `±30°`, `±90°` and `±150°` relative to the beam, with a multiplicity filter. Detector count/efficiency are not stated.

## Trigger and Coincidence Conditions

Coincidence data with a quoted `±20 ns` timing window were used for γ angular correlations. Hardware-trigger and event-count details are absent.

## Data Products

Partial level scheme, yrast extension, new sideband, mixed-link angular-correlation assignments and cross-isotone energy comparison.

## Nuclei and Bands Studied

[[132la]] and [[132la-chiral-vibration-doublet-candidate]]。

## Known Limitations

The article supplies relative rather than absolute spins. Common bandhead/configuration and chiral-vibration interpretation depend on systematics and model reasoning; no lifetimes or absolute strengths are reported.
