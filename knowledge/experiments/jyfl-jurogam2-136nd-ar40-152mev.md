# JYFL JUROGAM II `136Nd` Experiment

## Identity

High-statistics `136Nd` dataset used to establish five nearly degenerate linked band pairs. The paper publishes only the doublet subset; the complete level scheme and detailed angular-correlation analysis were deferred.

## Beam, Target and Reaction

`152-MeV` `40Ar` from the K130 cyclotron on a self-supporting enriched `100Mo` foil (`0.5 mg/cm²`), populating `136Nd` through `4n`. The source estimates about `100 mb` each for `135Nd/136Nd` within `480 mb` total.

## Detector Configuration

JUROGAM II, about `4.3%` efficiency at `1.3 MeV`; `5.1×10^10` prompt three-fold-and-higher events. GRAIN produced time-stamped 3D/4D histograms analyzed with RADWARE.

## Analysis Products

Coincidence links, relative band populations, DCO, `R_ac`, angular distributions and polarization support the partial level scheme and spin/parity assignments.

## Known Limitations

High-energy quadrupole links dominate weak low-energy in-band dipoles, leaving many dipoles unobserved. Four weak partners lack experimental `B(M1)/B(E2)`. Detailed angular-correlation and complete-scheme results are absent from this paper.
