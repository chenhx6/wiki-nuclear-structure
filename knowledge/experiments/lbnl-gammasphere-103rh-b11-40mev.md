# LBNL Gammasphere `103Rh` Experiment

## Identity

Kuti 2014 high-fold coincidence/DCO experiment used to extend `103Rh` Bands 3/6 and establish Bands 4/5.

## Beam, Target and Reaction

The LBNL 88-inch cyclotron delivered a `40-MeV` `11B` beam to an enriched self-supporting `96Zr` foil (`500 μg/cm²`), populating `103Rh` through the `4n` channel. Gammasphere recorded approximately `9×10^8` four-and-higher-fold events, sorted into two- and three-dimensional histograms with RADWARE.

## Detector Configuration

Gammasphere recorded prompt γ-ray coincidences. The source gives the high-fold event total but does not provide a detector-by-detector efficiency or live-time audit.

## Analysis Role

Coincidences and relative intensities extended Bands 3/6 and established Bands 4/5. With a stretched-quadrupole gate, the experiment expects `R_DCO≈1.0` for stretched quadrupole and `≈0.5` for stretched dipole transitions.

## Known Limitations

DCO ratios constrain multipolarity but not electric/magnetic character by themselves. The negative-parity assignments for Bands 4/5 additionally assume that comparable quadrupole and dipole links to a band are E2 and M1, respectively. The experiment reports no lifetimes or polarization.
