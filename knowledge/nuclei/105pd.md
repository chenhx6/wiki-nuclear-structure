# `105Pd`

## Identity

Odd-A `Z=46`, `N=59` nucleus in the A≈100 region.

## Production Reactions

The ingested wobbling source uses `96Zr(13C,4n)105Pd`.

## Bands

[[timar-2019-transverse-wobbling-105pd]] reports bands A/B as a one-neutron transverse-wobbling candidate up to `I=29/2`, supported by polarization-selected large `δ` and decreasing `E_wob`. Later [[nomura-2022-questioning-wobbling-ibfm]] provides a model-based challenge and notes conflicting experimental mixing ratios.

## Competing Interpretations

Transverse wobbling, TiP and an IBFM particle-core description remain to be compared with consistent band labels and transition data.
