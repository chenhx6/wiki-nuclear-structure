# `161Lu`

## Identity

Odd-proton `Z=71`, `N=90` nucleus in the A≈160 TSD region.

## Production Reactions

`139La(28Si,6n)161Lu` at 175 MeV.

## Bands

[[bringel-2005-wobbling-161lu]] reports TSD1 and a weaker TSD2 and says isotope systematics strongly suggest `n_w=0/1`. Spins/energies are tentative and connecting-transition electromagnetic properties were not determined.

## Competing Interpretations

Wobbling remains a systematics-based interpretation; configuration changes and neutron crossings are alternatives.

## Open Questions

- Is there a measurable second-phonon partner?
