# `104Mo`

## Identity

`Z=42, N=62` even-even neutron-rich nucleus. Musangu 2021 combines an extended collective level scheme with a proposed negative-parity soft chiral-vibrational pair.

## Production Reactions

- Prompt product of `252Cf` spontaneous fission: [[lbnl-gammasphere-cf252-fission-mo104-106]].

## Established Level Structure

The ground-state, one-phonon γ and two-phonon γ bands are revised/extended in Fig.1 and Table I. The earlier `601.6-keV` two-phonon-band line is replaced by `597.3 keV`. Additional Bands 4-8 contain negative-parity, unassigned-parity and higher-phonon candidates.

## Bands

- [[104mo-bands-4-5-soft-chiral-vibration-candidate]]: negative-parity pair separated by about `60 keV` over the observed common-spin range.
- Band 8: tentative three-phonon γ-vibrational assignment, with a quasiparticle-band alternative retained.

## Deformation and Collective Features

Low-lying one- and two-phonon γ structures motivate γ softness. TPSM uses `ε=0.24`, `γ=20°`; this is a model input, not a direct deformation measurement.

## Competing Interpretations

The Band-4/Band-5 relation may reflect chiral orientation motion, configuration mixing or another collective vibration. The predicted collective connecting E2 transitions are not observed and no lifetimes are available.

## Open Questions

- Do absolute in-band and interband strengths confirm collective reorientation rather than different configurations?
- Is Band 8 a three-phonon γ vibration or a quasiparticle band?
- Does the predicted four-neutron crossing near `I=17` occur experimentally?
