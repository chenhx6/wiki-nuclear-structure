# `196Tl`

## Identity

`Z=81, N=115` odd-odd nucleus. Guo 2022 identifies three favorable minima and recommends a partner search around a previously observed rotational sequence.

## Model Structure

A*/E*/H* have `(β,γ)=(0.13,34.88°)`, `(0.18,37.26°)` and `(0.12,42.75°)`, all with unpaired `πh9/2⊗νi13/2^-1`. The authors translate these into a prediction of three doublets/six `ΔI=1` bands.

## Evidence Boundary

Only one relevant experimental band is included in the secondary systematics; no partner is established here. Three minima are not six observed bands.

## Production Reactions

No reaction is established from the theory source; its observed reference band comes from cited earlier work.

## Bands

No band page is created. A*/E*/H* are model states, and the three corresponding doublets remain predictions.

## Competing Interpretations

The three shallow configuration minima may represent shape coexistence or mixing rather than three independently observable chiral systems.
