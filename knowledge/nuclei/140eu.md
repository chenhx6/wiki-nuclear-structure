# `140Eu`

## Identity

`Z=63, N=77` odd-odd nucleus. The current entry preserves Hecht 2003's five-band high-spin scheme and its unresolved chiral-twin versus triaxial-shape-coexistence interpretation.

## Production Reactions

- `92Mo(51V,2pn)140Eu` at 205 MeV with Yale YRAST Ball: [[yale-yrast-ball-140eu-v51-205mev]].

## Established Level Structure

[[hecht-2003-chirality-shape-coexistence-140eu]] reports five bands and 69 transitions above low-lying 125-ms `5−` and about 300-ns isomers. Bands 1/2 are linked negative-parity structures; Bands 3/4 are linked positive-parity structures; Band 5 has an uncertain connection through an unobserved 20.5-keV transition.

## Bands

- [[140eu-bands-1-2-doublet-candidate]]: assigned mixed `π(g7/2,d5/2)⊗νh11/2`; possible chiral twins or shape-coexisting structures.
- [[140eu-bands-3-4-doublet-candidate]]: assigned `πh11/2⊗νh11/2`; possible chiral twins or shape-coexisting structures.

## Deformation and Collective Features

TRS calculations predict two close minima around `β≈0.2, γ≈±25°` over a wide rotational-frequency interval. They provide the basis both for possible aplanar rotation and for the competing same-configuration shape-coexistence interpretation; they are not direct shape measurements.

## Competing Interpretations

Both linked pairs can be read as chiral-twin candidates, but the same calculated potential-energy surfaces allow distinct positive- and negative-`γ` shapes. The source does not select between them. Missing signature partners, configuration mixing and absent lifetime-separated strengths remain active counter-evidence.

## Key Observables

- linked level scheme, coincidence spectra and excitation-function/x-ray isotope assignment;
- gated angular distributions, DCO ratios and Compton asymmetry;
- alignment and branching-derived `B(M1)/B(E2)` ratios;
- TRS/Routhian and triaxial particle-rotor comparisons.

## Open Questions

- Do absolute intraband and interband strengths show the predicted abrupt chiral change or smooth shape-coexistence evolution near `I≈15ℏ`?
- Are the missing signature partners of Bands 2 and 4 merely below sensitivity?
- Can later data stabilize Band 5's connection and interpretation?
