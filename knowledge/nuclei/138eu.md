# `138Eu`

## Identity

`Z=63`、`N=75` 的 odd-odd nucleus。当前入口保存 Hecht 2001 首次报告的新 `ΔI=1` partner sequence 及其 chiral-twin-candidate interpretation。

## Production Reactions

- `106Cd(35Cl,2pn)138Eu`，150 MeV，Daresbury Eurogam Phase I：[[daresbury-eurogam-138eu-cl35-150mev]]。

## Established Level Structure

[[hecht-2001-chiral-symmetry-breaking-136pm-138eu]] Fig. 1 给出新带、已知 yrast band 及两者间多条 links；Fig. 2(a) 显示以 164、299、351 和 383-keV transitions 双门得到的新带谱。若干 spins 以括号标记，仍为 tentative/systematics-dependent。

## Bands

- [[138eu-chiral-twin-candidate-pair]]：已知 yrast 带与新 `ΔI=1` 带；作者赋相同 `πh11/2⊗νh11/2` 组态并提出 chiral-twin candidate。

## Deformation and Collective Features

TRS-derived `(ε2,ε4,γ)=(0.202,0.032,-24°)` at `ℏω≈0.25 MeV` is used as the 3D TAC input. The calculated aplanar solution approaches `φ=0°` around `ℏω≈0.35 MeV`, which the authors interpret as termination of the chiral interval.

## Competing Interpretations

The new band remains about `300 keV` above the corresponding yrast states. Hecht 2001 relates this to left/right tunnelling and argues that neighbour signature splittings and other excitations are larger; it does not provide a quantum calculation of tunnelling or an exhaustive alternative-model comparison.

## Key Observables

- Fig. 1 partial level scheme and Fig. 2(a) double-gated spectrum；
- DCO-based relative spin/multipolarity assignments；
- transition-energy, alignment and `B(M1)/B(E2)` similarity；
- 3D TAC tilt-angle and ratio calculations。
