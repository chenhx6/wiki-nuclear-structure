# `133Ba`

## Identity

Odd-neutron `Z=56`, `N=77` nucleus in the A≈130 region.

## Production Reactions

The ingested source uses `124Sn(13C,4n)133Ba` at 48 MeV.

## Bands

[[rojeeta-devi-2021-multiphonon-transverse-wobbling-133ba]] reports `n_ω=0,1,2` transverse-wobbling candidates based on a hole-like `νh11/2` configuration. Several links have DCO/polarization support, while two low-energy links remain uncharacterized.

## Competing Interpretations

The multiphonon hierarchy competes with signature-partner and other particle-core structures when low-energy links are not characterized.
