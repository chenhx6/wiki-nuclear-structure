# `107Rh`

## Identity

`Z=45, N=62` odd-mass nucleus. Peng and Chen 2022 includes it as the neutron-rich end of a `102-107Rh` 3D-TAC CDFT trend.

## Production Reactions

No experiment or reaction is reported in the 2022 theory paper.

## Established Level Structure

No experimental doublet energy difference is available in Fig.6, and the calculated systematics are not a substitute for a measured band identity.

## Bands

## Deformation and Collective Features

For `πg9/2^-3⊗ν[h11/2(gd)^11]`, PC-PK1 3D-TAC CDFT predicts the earliest aplanar onset in the chain, `ℏωcrit=0.34 MeV`. The mechanism is the reduced incremental short-axis and increased medium-axis contribution of the added `(gd)` neutrons.

## Competing Interpretations

This is a theory-only prediction with no pair splitting or critical-kink measurement. Pairing and quantum partner-band dynamics are absent.
