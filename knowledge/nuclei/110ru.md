# `110Ru`

## Identity

`Z=44, N=66` even-even nucleus. Luo 2009 interprets two close negative-parity composite sequences as zero- and one-phonon soft chiral-vibrational bands.

## Production Reactions

- Prompt product of `252Cf` spontaneous fission: [[gammasphere-cf252-fission-ru108-112]].

## Established Level Structure

Bands 4-5 and 6-7 are connected `ΔI=1` composite sequences in Fig.1. Same-spin differences, `S(I)` and branching-derived ratios are compared in Figs.3-4 and Table 1.

## Bands

- [[110ru-chiral-vibration-doublet-candidate]]: Bands 4-5 zero-phonon and 6-7 one-phonon assignment.

## Deformation and Collective Features

Prior IBM/γ-band context favors a triaxial rotor. TAC gives `γ≈22°` and an orientation-soft planar solution; RPA gives a low-lying `≈300 keV` collective orientation phonon.

## Competing Interpretations

The composite sequences could in principle use different two-neutron configurations, although model ratios disfavor the tested alternative. Pure-M1/branching assumptions and absent absolute strengths remain experimental limitations.

## Open Questions

- Do lifetime-separated matrix elements confirm the proposed zero-/one-phonon relationship?
- Does a large-amplitude model retain the same interpretation beyond harmonic RPA?
