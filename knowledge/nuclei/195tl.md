# `195Tl`

## Identity

`Z=81, N=114` odd-mass nucleus in which earlier work reported MχD candidates. Guo 2022 recalculates only a lower-quasiparticle sector.

## Model Structure

I*/J* are positive-parity `πh9/2⊗ν(fp)^1i13/2^-1` candidates; K* is a negative-parity `πh9/2⊗ν(i13/2)^-2` candidate. The resulting count is two positive plus one negative predicted doublet.

## Evidence Boundary

The known five-quasiparticle states cannot be provided by the present calculation. Previously reported bands and Fig.4 ratios are secondary inputs, while I*/J* candidate pairs remain experimentally unreported in this source.

## Production Reactions

No production reaction is established from this calculation; original spectroscopy is outside the read scope.

## Bands

No band page is created. The source's I*/J*/K* labels denote minima, not laboratory rotational bands.

## Competing Interpretations

The calculated three-quasiparticle candidates cannot replace the separate five-quasiparticle interpretation of known bands; `(fp)` orbital ambiguity also remains.
