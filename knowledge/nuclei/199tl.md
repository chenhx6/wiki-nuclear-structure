# `199Tl`

## Identity

`Z=81, N=118` odd-mass nucleus used as a negative control in Guo 2022's Tl-isotope screening.

## Model Result

Although an experimental three-quasiparticle `πh9/2⊗ν(i13/2)^-2` band is cited, the PK1 constrained calculation finds no suitable triaxial minimum and removes `199Tl` from its candidate set.

## Evidence Boundary

This is a model-specific null under no pairing and the selected constrained configurations, not a universal proof that chirality is impossible in `199Tl`.

## Production Reactions

No reaction is established from this theory source; the cited three-quasiparticle band is secondary.

## Bands

No band page is created. A known high-`j` band is mentioned but does not map to a favorable minimum in this calculation.

## Competing Interpretations

Pairing, another functional or a different configuration space could change the absence of a suitable minimum.

## Open Questions
