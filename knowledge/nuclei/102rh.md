# `102Rh`

## Identity

`Z=45, N=57` odd-odd nucleus. In Peng and Chen 2022 it is the low-neutron control for the `102-107Rh` 3D-TAC CDFT systematics.

## Production Reactions

The 2022 theory paper imports earlier experimental data but does not report a reaction or new experiment. Return to its Ref.11 before recording production metadata.

## Established Level Structure

Previously published yrast/side-band energies, ratios and inertias are plotted as secondary data. The pair reaches only a minimum separation near `460 keV` in Fig.6 and was previously judged inconsistent with static chirality over the observed spins.

## Bands

No stable band page is created from the secondary plots in Peng and Chen 2022.

## Deformation and Collective Features

For `πg9/2^-3⊗ν[h11/2(gd)^6]`, PC-PK1 3D-TAC CDFT remains planar through `ℏω=0.75 MeV`; convergence fails above that point.

## Competing Interpretations

The calculation does not support static chirality in the observed range but does not exclude chiral vibration about the planar solution. Omitted pairing likely contributes to overestimated `I(ω)`, alignment and `J(1)`.
