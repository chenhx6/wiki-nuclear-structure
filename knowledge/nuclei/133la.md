# `133La`

## Identity

Odd-proton `Z=57`, `N=76` nucleus in the A≈130 region.

## Production Reactions

The ingested experiment uses `126Te(11B,4n)133La` at 52 MeV.

## Bands

[[biswas-2019-longitudinal-wobbling-133la]] reports E2-dominated interband links and increasing `E_wob`, interpreted as the first longitudinal wobbling observation. The proposed early `(dg)` proton-pair alignment is model based. [[nomura-2022-questioning-wobbling-ibfm]] supplies a later alternative description.

## Competing Interpretations

Longitudinal wobbling, signature-partner structure and IBFM particle-core coupling require common-input comparison.

## Open Questions

- How sensitive are the link mixing ratios to assumed `σ/I=0.3`?
- Can absolute lifetimes distinguish the pair-alignment mechanism?
