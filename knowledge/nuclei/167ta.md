# `167Ta`

## Identity

Odd-proton `Z=73`, `N=94` nucleus in the A≈160 TSD region.

## Production Reactions

`120Sn(51V,4n)167Ta` at 235 MeV.

## Bands

[[hartley-2009-wobbling-167ta]] reports TSD1/TSD2 with Lu-like rotational and decay topology and suggests wobbling. The required E2 dominance was not measured: the paper assumes 90% E2 from `163Lu`.

## Competing Interpretations

Unfavoured signature, wobbling and an SD-to-TSD interaction scenario are discussed.

## Open Questions

- What is the actual link mixing ratio/polarization?
- Do lifetimes verify the proposed SD-to-TSD interaction?
