# `104Ag`

## Identity

`Z=47, N=57` odd-odd particle-hole nucleus. Wang 2013 maps coexisting low-lying multiplets, electric-quadrupole and magnetic rotation, and a negative-parity chiral-doublet candidate.

## Production Reactions

- `97Mo(11B,4n)104Ag` at `50 MeV`: [[ciae-hi13-104ag-b11-50mev]].

## Established Level Structure

The revised scheme contains more than 40 new γ rays arranged as Bands A-F. A/B are negative-parity linked sequences; C is a positive-parity low-lying multiplet-like structure; D/E are positive-parity electric-quadrupole bands; F is a positive-parity magnetic-rotation band.

## Bands

- [[104ag-bands-a-b-chiral-doublet-candidate]]: `πg9/2^-1⊗νh11/2` candidate pair.
- [[104ag-bands-d-e-electric-quadrupole-rotation]]: proposed `d5/2/g7/2` configurations with mixing.
- [[104ag-band-f-magnetic-rotation]]: inherited/proposed four-quasiparticle magnetic band.

## Deformation and Collective Features

A/B nondegeneracy is interpreted through γ-soft chiral vibration by analogy, not a new deformation calculation. D/E and F demonstrate coexistence of electric and magnetic rotational structures in the same nucleus.

## Competing Interpretations

A/B could be same-configuration ordinary partner/mixed bands; no lifetimes or dedicated chiral calculation establish handedness. D/E mixing and F configuration are systematics/prior-model dependent.

## Open Questions

- Do absolute A/B in-/out-of-band strengths satisfy a common chiral model?
- Is the `N=57` lower-border claim stable after later candidate discoveries?
- Can one calculation describe A/B chirality, D/E configuration mixing and F shears behavior consistently?
