# `127Xe`

## Identity

Odd-neutron `Z=54`, `N=73` nucleus in the A≈130 region.

## Production Reactions

The ingested experiment uses `122Sn(9Be,4n)127Xe` at 48 MeV.

## Bands

[[chakraborty-2020-multiphonon-longitudinal-wobbling-127xe]] assigns four negative-parity bands to longitudinal-wobbling/signature structures. The one-phonon links have E2 evidence of uneven quality; the second-phonon assignment has a nonstandard decay pattern. [[nomura-2022-questioning-wobbling-ibfm]] supplies an alternative model description.

## Competing Interpretations

Longitudinal wobbling, signature partnership, quasi-γ-like decay and IBFM particle-core coupling remain relevant.

## Open Questions

- Is band 4 a genuine second-phonon band?
- Can contaminated/ambiguous links be remeasured with polarization and lifetimes?
