# `167Lu`

## Identity

Odd-proton `Z=71`, `N=96` nucleus in the A≈160 TSD region.

## Production Reactions

`123Sb(48Ca,4n)167Lu` at 203 MeV.

## Bands

[[amro-2003-wobbling-167lu]] connects TSD2 to `πi13/2` TSD1 and reports large inferred E2 fractions/relative strengths. Polarization was unavailable, so selection of the large-`abs(δ)` branch is model/systematics dependent.

## Competing Interpretations

Unfavoured signature and three-quasiparticle alternatives were rejected by the authors using energy/alignment/model comparisons.

## Open Questions

- Can direct polarization independently select the link branch?
- Do lifetimes confirm the inferred relative strengths?
