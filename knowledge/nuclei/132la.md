# `132La`

## Identity

`Z=57`、`N=75` 的 odd-odd nucleus。当前页面保存 Starosta 2001 新 sideband、relative-spin/positive-parity assignment 与 soft chiral-vibration interpretation。

## Production Reactions

- `123Sb(13C,4n)132La`，64 MeV，Stony Brook LINAC：[[stony-brook-linac-132la-c13-64mev]]。

## Established Level Structure

Starosta 2001 Fig. 2 把新 `ΔI=1` sideband 与已知 `πh11/2⊗νh11/2` yrast band 并列；mixed `M1/E2` links 支持 positive parity 与 relative spins。共同 `I=9ℏ` bandhead 由四核系统学选择。

## Bands

- [[132la-chiral-vibration-doublet-candidate]]：约 `0.37 MeV` 位移的 yrast/sideband pair。

## Deformation and Collective Features

3D TAC Table I gives `ε=0.175`, `γ=32°`, `θ=65°`, `φ=36°` and `E_b=12 keV` at `ℏω=0.35 MeV`. Starosta interprets this nondegenerate pair as a soft chiral vibration; the deformation/barrier are model outputs.

## Competing Interpretations

Quasiparticle and γ-vibrational alternatives are disfavoured by regional energy scales but not compared in one quantum model. The chiral vibration is inferred from systematics rather than directly calculated.

## Key Observables

- Fig. 2 partial level scheme and mixed links；
- roughly constant `≈0.37 MeV` displacement；
- even/odd-spin interleaving and relative-spin pattern；
- TAC aplanar angles/barrier and predicted transition strengths。
