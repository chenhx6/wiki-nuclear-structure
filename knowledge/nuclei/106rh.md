# `106Rh`

## Identity

`Z=45, N=61` odd-odd nucleus with previously reported chiral candidates. Peng and Chen 2022 supplies a self-consistent isotope-chain calculation for the one-`νh11/2` configuration.

## Production Reactions

The theory paper imports experimental data and reports no reaction. Original Refs.6 and 19 must be consulted for experiment metadata.

## Established Level Structure

Earlier yrast/side-band energies, ratios and moments appear as secondary comparisons. Pair separations are around `200-300 keV` in the plotted spin range.

## Bands

No stable band page is created from the secondary plots; the current page records only the 2022 model-systematics boundary.

## Deformation and Collective Features

For `πg9/2^-3⊗ν[h11/2(gd)^10]`, 3D-TAC CDFT gives `ℏωcrit=0.46 MeV`. Experimental and calculated `I(ω)` kink positions are close, and `I`, alignment, `J(1)` and `B(M1)/B(E2)` are broadly reproduced.

## Competing Interpretations

Mean-field agreement does not calculate the side band or tunnelling. Pairing and quantum collective dynamics remain absent, so the experimental doublet is not independently confirmed by the paper.
