# `193Tl`

## Identity

`Z=81, N=112` odd-mass nucleus. Earlier literature reports three negative-parity bands, but Guo 2022 contributes a constrained-RMF candidate map rather than new spectroscopy.

## Model Structure

Five minima are selected: three positive-parity `πh9/2⊗ν(fp)^1i13/2^-1` cases and two negative-parity `πh9/2⊗ν(i13/2)^-2` cases. Here `(fp)` deliberately leaves the `3p3/2`, `3p1/2` and `2f5/2` spectator orbital unresolved.

## Evidence Boundary

The predicted five-pair MχD count is not an observed band count. Existing negative-parity structures are secondary context and require original-source checking before detailed reuse.

[[bark-2024-investigations-nuclear-chirality-ithembalabs]] adds a secondary reconstruction of three observed `πh9/2⊗νi13/2^-2` negative-parity bands. It retains two explanations: two chiral systems with an unobserved fourth band, or a `γ≈-45°` intermediate-axis chiral pair competing with a `γ≈-90°` long-axis planar band. The authors favor the latter but do not exclude the former (BK24-14; PDF pp.20-22, Figs.28-29).

## Production Reactions

The iThemba review lists `160Gd(37Cl,4n)` at `167 MeV` and `181Ta(18O,6n)` at `105 MeV` as secondary experiment metadata. Return to the original article before precision reuse (BK24-7; PDF p.9, Table 1).

## Bands

No band page is created. Three earlier negative-parity bands are mentioned, but their scheme and pairing are not re-established here.

## Competing Interpretations

The five minima may be reorganized by pairing or mixing, and unresolved `(fp)` occupations prevent orbital-specific configuration claims.

## Open Questions

- Which, if any, experimental bands map uniquely to the five minima?
- Can partner-resolved lifetimes and a rotational model distinguish the three- versus five-pair interpretations?
