# `105Ag`

## Identity

`Z=47, N=58` odd-mass nucleus. Timár 2007 separates a null search for the preferred positive-parity three-quasiparticle Band E from a different negative-parity D/G chiral-candidate pair.

## Production Reactions

- `100Mo(10B,5n)105Ag` at 58 and 64 MeV: [[stony-brook-linac-105ag-b10-58-64mev]].

## Established Level Structure

Timár 2007 reconstructs partial Bands A–G, newly observes F/G, extends D and corrects the placement/spins of Band E. High-spin assignments in D/F/G are partly tentative and should be traced to Fig.2/Table I.

## Bands

- [[105ag-band-e-three-quasiparticle-reference]]: corrected positive-parity `πg9/2⊗ν(h11/2)^2` target band; no side band above ≈0.1 population ratio.
- [[105ag-bands-d-g-chiral-doublet-candidate]]: linked negative-parity natural-parity pair with ≈70-keV separation and chiral fingerprints, but unresolved configuration mixing.

## Deformation and Collective Features

TRS comparison supports the assigned configurations and is used to infer axial/γ-rigid behavior for Band E and a triaxial context for the C/D/G family. The latter does not establish an aplanar D/G wave function.

## Competing Interpretations

D/G may reflect chiral coupling or simple mixing between `g7/2`- and `d5/2`-based natural-parity configurations. Band-E partner absence is sensitivity-bounded. No source-level claim establishes chirality for the nucleus as a whole.

## Key Observables

- coincidences, links and DCO ratios;
- experimental Routhians and alignments;
- branching-derived `B(M1)/B(E2)`;
- same-spin separation and `S(I)`;
- ≈0.1 Band-E partner-search sensitivity.

## Open Questions

- Are D/G absolute in-band/interband matrix elements compatible with a quantum chiral model rather than configuration mixing?
- Does a Band-E partner exist below the reported population threshold?
- How do later `103,104Rh` and `103,104Ag` systematics change the candidate assignment?
