# `74As`

## Identity

`Z=33, N=41` odd-odd nucleus. Xiao 2022 establishes two positive-parity and one negative-parity high-spin bands and interprets them as coexisting chiral-candidate and octupole-correlated structures.

## Production Reactions

- `74Ge(4He,1p3n)74As` at `58.6/62.6 MeV`: [[ithembalabs-afrodite-74as-he4-58-62mev]].

## Established Level Structure

Band 1 is extended to `(16+)`; Band 2 is established from `9+` to `(14+)` and linked to Band 1 five times. Band 3 is a negative-parity sequence through `14−`. Band-1 spins are one unit above Ref.32, supported by As isotope systematics. An unobserved `≈11.8-keV` gap remains below the `55-keV` branch.

## Bands

- [[74as-bands-1-2-chiral-doublet-candidate]]: common `πg9/2⊗νg9/2` positive-parity candidate pair.
- [[74as-band-3-octupole-correlated-negative-parity]]: tentative `π(f5/2/p3/2)⊗νg9/2` negative-parity band connected to Band 1 by three E1 transitions.

## Competing Interpretations

Bands 1/2 have a fitted aplanar TPRM description, but the neutron angular momentum is mixed across all axes and no absolute partner strengths are measured. Band 1/3 support octupole correlations through E1/energy systematics but differ from stable-octupole `224Th`; Band 3's proton orbital remains ambiguous.

## Key Observables

Coincidence links, ADO, polarization asymmetry, energy separation, `S(I)`, `B(M1)/B(E2)`, `B(E1)/B(E2)` and `δE`.

## Open Questions

- Do partner-resolved lifetimes preserve the apparent electromagnetic similarity of Bands 1/2?
- Can a less fitted model reproduce the pair and the nonideal neutron geometry simultaneously?
- Do absolute E1 strengths and a reflection-asymmetric calculation confirm dynamic octupole correlations without stable deformation?
