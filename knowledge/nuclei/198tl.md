# `198Tl`

## Identity

`Z=81, N=117` odd-odd nucleus with a previously reported candidate chiral pair. Guo 2022 predicts a second favorable minimum.

## Model Structure

B*/F* have `(β,γ)=(0.11,39.43°)` and `(0.16,43.32°)`, both with unpaired `πh9/2⊗νi13/2^-1`. The earlier particle-rotor estimate `γ≈44°` is quoted as agreement with the calculated region.

## Evidence Boundary

The measured pair and rotor comparison are imported from earlier work. Guo 2022 supplies a two-minimum MχD prediction but no new band, spectrum or electromagnetic calculation.

[[bark-2024-investigations-nuclear-chirality-ithembalabs]] summarizes the earlier `πh9/2⊗νi13/2^-1` pair: partner energies differ by about `500 keV`, while alignments, moments of inertia and branching-derived ratios are similar. QTR with residual proton-neutron interaction at `γ≈44°` reproduces selected trends and supports aplanar geometry; this remains a model-assisted secondary classification, not exact degeneracy (BK24-11; PDF pp.13-16, Figs.16-19).

## Production Reactions

The iThemba review lists `197Au(α,3n)` at `40 MeV` for both AFRODITE γ-ray and Orsay electron-spectrometer data. The original articles remain the precision source (BK24-7; PDF p.9, Table 1).

## Bands

No new band page is created. The reported pair is secondary context, and B*/F* label theoretical minima.

## Competing Interpretations

Agreement of calculated `γ` with a prior rotor input is model-to-model consistency, not independent shape measurement or proof of a second pair.

## Open Questions

- Is a second pair associated with the F* minimum observable?
- Do absolute partner strengths support both the reported and predicted systems?
