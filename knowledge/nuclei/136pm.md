# `136Pm`

## Identity

`Z=61`、`N=75` 的 odd-odd nucleus。当前入口聚焦 Hecht 2001 的 positive-parity yrast/new-band pair；自旋、组态与 chirality 解释均保持 source-attributed 和 unreviewed。

## Production Reactions

- `116Sn(24Mg,p3n)136Pm`，130 和 135 MeV，Yale YRAST Ball：[[yale-yrast-ball-136pm-mg24-130-135mev]]。

Starosta 2001 对 130-MeV Yale experiment 印作 `116Sn(24Mg,4n)136Pm`，该道电荷数不自洽；当前以 source conflict 保存，而不是静默覆盖 Hecht 2001 的 `p3n`。

## Established Level Structure

[[hecht-2001-chiral-symmetry-breaking-136pm-138eu]] Fig. 1 给出 partial level scheme：一条新 `ΔI=1` sequence 通过 mixed-dipole 与 E2 links 接入已知 yrast structure。新带的 parenthesized spins 与 bandhead assignment 依赖 Starosta 2001 systematics，当前不当作独立确定值。

[[starosta-2001-n75-chiral-vibrations]] Fig. 2 给出相关 yrast/sideband structure，并用 angular-correlation timing 指派 mixed `M1/E2` links、positive parity 和 relative spins。共同 `I=9ℏ` bandhead 是四核系统学选择。

## Bands

- [[136pm-chiral-twin-candidate-pair]]：已知 yrast 带与本文新带；作者赋相同 `πh11/2⊗νh11/2` 组态并提出 chiral-twin candidate。
- Starosta 2001 把同一来源谱系中的 pair 解释为约 `0.29 MeV` 位移的 soft chiral vibration；Hecht 2001 随后增加 selected polarization 与更直接的 candidate comparison。两文非独立重复。

## Deformation and Collective Features

TRS at `ℏω≈0.25 MeV` supplies `(ε2,ε4,γ)=(0.194,0.028,-25°)` to 3D TAC. This is a model input, not a measured static deformation. TAC gives an aplanar interval before returning toward principal-axis rotation around `ℏω≈0.42 MeV`.

## Competing Interpretations

Hecht 2001 argues against a simple unfavoured proton-signature assignment using energy systematics, and regards quasiparticle/γ-vibrational states as higher. Those alternatives were not all calculated against the same electromagnetic data. The `≈300 keV` splitting is interpreted through tunnelling, not directly observed as a left/right coordinate.

Starosta 2001 提供 chiral-vibration/island 的系统学解释，但 collective vibrational eigenstate 未被计算；其 printed reaction 与 detector count 还和 Hecht 2001 存在 metadata conflict。

## Key Observables

- partial level scheme, in-band/crossover/interband links and DCO assignments；
- 364-keV in-band and 595/684-keV interband polarization asymmetries；
- alignment and branching-derived `B(M1)/B(E2)` similarity between the two bands；
- 3D TAC tilt angles and calculated ratio trend。

## Open Questions

- Starosta 2001 的 isotone systematics 对本核 bandhead spins 和 chiral-vibration classification 给出何种额外约束？
- 是否存在 lifetimes、absolute strengths、mixing ratios 或 configuration-sensitive measurements，可检验共同组态与 tunnelling interpretation？
- 后续完整 level scheme 是否确认 Fig. 1 中全部 tentative spins/links？
