# `183Au`

## Identity

Odd-proton `Z=79`, `N=104` nucleus in the A≈180 region.

## Production Reactions

`169Tm(20Ne,6n)183Au` at 146 MeV.

## Bands

[[nandi-2020-multiple-transverse-wobbling-183au]] reports positive- and negative-parity one-phonon transverse-wobbling candidates. The experiment measures contrasting `E_wob` trends; the inference of triaxial shape coexistence remains model dependent.

## Competing Interpretations

Signature-partner controls, transverse-wobbling turning behavior and triaxial shape coexistence require separate evidentiary treatment.
