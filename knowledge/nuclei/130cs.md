# `130Cs`

## Identity

`Z=55`、`N=75` 的 odd-odd nucleus。当前页面保存 Starosta 2001 新 sideband、相对自旋/positive-parity assignment 与 soft chiral-vibration interpretation。

## Production Reactions

- `124Sn(10B,4n)130Cs`，47 MeV，Stony Brook LINAC：[[stony-brook-linac-130cs-b10-47mev]]。

## Established Level Structure

Starosta 2001 Fig. 2 把新 `ΔI=1` sideband 置于已知 `πh11/2⊗νh11/2` yrast band 左侧。两带由 mixed `M1/E2` links 相连，支持同宇称与相对自旋序列；共同 `I=9ℏ` bandhead 来自系统学。

## Bands

- [[130cs-chiral-vibration-doublet-candidate]]：约 `0.25 MeV` 位移的 yrast/sideband pair。

## Deformation and Collective Features

3D TAC Table I gives `ε=0.16`, `γ=39°`, `θ=65°`, `φ=32°` and a shallow `E_b=3 keV` at `ℏω=0.30 MeV`. These are model results. Starosta interprets the shallow/soft case as a precursor chiral vibration rather than stable near-degenerate chirality.

## Competing Interpretations

Quasiparticle and conventional γ-vibrational excitations are argued to lie higher, but no unified competing calculation is shown. Common configuration and collective chiral vibration remain author/model interpretations.

## Key Observables

- Fig. 2 partial level scheme and mixed `M1/E2` links；
- roughly constant `≈0.25 MeV` pair displacement；
- even/odd-spin interleaving and common relative-spin pattern；
- Table I aplanar TAC angles/barrier/transition strengths。

## Open Questions

- 后续 lifetime/absolute-strength measurements 是否支持共同组态与 chiral-vibration assignment？
- `I=9` absolute bandhead 与 Fig. 2 links 是否由独立实验确认？
