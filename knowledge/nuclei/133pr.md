# `133Pr`

## Identity

`Z=59, N=74` odd-proton nucleus. Singh 2016 uses its decoupled low-Ω `πh11/2` yrast band as a shape-polarization control against the high-Ω neutron band in `131Ce`.

## Production Reactions

- `118Sn(19F,4n)133Pr` at `92 MeV`: [[iuac-gda-133pr-f19-rdds]].

## Established Level Structure

The negative-parity yrast sequence contains the `310,552,709,813,864-keV` transitions through `31/2−`. A positive-parity band feeds the `23/2−` state through a `682-keV` transition; the source measures that feeding level because it constrains the yrast decay curves.

## Bands

- [[133pr-negative-parity-yrast-reference-sequence]]: decoupled `πh11/2` lifetime/collectivity reference.

## Lifetimes and Transition Strengths

Finite yrast lifetimes are `71(+6/−5), 3.9(+0.3/−0.2), 1.43(+0.10/−0.14) ps` for `15/2−,19/2−,23/2−`, with `Q_t≈3.55,3.53,3.09 eb`; the two higher states give limits. A long about-`1 ns` cascade side feeding is required at `15/2−`.

## Competing Interpretations

The data are compatible with approximately stable collectivity and at most a marginal decrease. Near-prolate shape is a TRS/rotor/single-particle interpretation on a γ-soft surface, valid before the reported band crossing; it is not direct γ measurement or chirality evidence.

## Open Questions

- Do post-crossing lifetimes retain the low-Ω proton shape polarization?
- Can coincidence-gated lifetime data reduce the long-side-feeding dependence?
- How stable is the `πh11/2` identity under configuration mixing near the crossing?
