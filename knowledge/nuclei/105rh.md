# `105Rh`

## Identity

`Z=45, N=60` odd-mass nucleus. Earlier literature reports chiral/MχD candidates; Peng and Chen 2022 studies one `νh11/2` configuration family in isotope-chain 3D-TAC CDFT.

## Production Reactions

The 2022 theory paper reports no experiment. Production metadata must be recovered from the original experimental Refs.8-9 before reuse.

## Established Level Structure

The published yrast and side-band data are secondary inputs in Figs.3-7. Experimental pair separation reaches about `650 keV`, substantially larger than the `103,104,106Rh` `200-300-keV` scale cited by the paper.

## Bands

No band page is created from this theory paper because it does not establish a new experimental identity or calculate the side-band spectrum.

## Deformation and Collective Features

For `πg9/2^-3⊗ν[h11/2(gd)^9]`, the self-consistent aplanar solution begins at calculated `ℏωcrit=0.49 MeV`.

## Competing Interpretations

The model reproduces the available `I(ω)`, alignment and `J(1)` trends reasonably, but the experimental critical-frequency kink is identified as a future verification need. Other reported `105Rh` configurations are outside the single-`h11/2` calculation.

## Open Questions

- Is the predicted `0.49-MeV` planar-to-aplanar kink present in configuration-resolved data?
- Does the isotope trend survive pairing and beyond-mean-field side-band calculations?
