# `104Rh`

## Identity

`Z=45, N=59` odd-odd nucleus. Suzuki 2008 measures five lifetimes in one member of a proposed negative-parity chiral doublet.

## Production Reactions

- `11B(96Zr,3n)104Rh` at `330 MeV`: [[atlas-gammasphere-rdds-103rh-104rh-zr96-330mev]].

## Established Level Structure

The imported doublet scheme has near-constant `S(I)` in Fig.1. The assigned `πg9/2^-1⊗νh11/2` bandhead is `8−`, consistent in the authors' semiclassical interpretation with perpendicular valence angular momenta.

## Bands

- [[104rh-chiral-doublet-candidate]]: negative-parity two-quasiparticle candidate pair; only one sequence receives lifetime measurements here.

## Lifetimes and Transition Strengths

For `9−–13−`, lifetimes are `6.21(45), 1.25(9), 1.34(12), 1.05(10), 0.71(19) ps`. Corresponding `B(M1)` values decline from `2.3(2)` to `0.42(15) μN²`; `B(E2)` is tabulated for `10−–13−` as `0.063(17), 0.093(20), 0.039(10), 0.067(25) e²b²`.

## Competing Interpretations

The odd-even variation lies in `B(E2)`, not `B(M1)`, and is not explained by the source. The cited particle-rotor behavior is not a dedicated calculation of both `104Rh` partner bands. A shape change remains an alternative.

Peng and Chen 2022 predicts planar-to-aplanar onset at `ℏωcrit=0.55 MeV` for the assigned one-`νh11/2` family and finds the experimental `I(ω)` kink nearby. However, neglect of pairing leads the calculation to overestimate `I(ω)`, alignment, `J(1)` and `B(M1)/B(E2)`, and the side band/splitting remains beyond mean field.

## Open Questions

- Do partner-band and higher-spin lifetimes converge with the measured sequence?
- Can a dedicated quantum calculation reproduce the `B(E2)` staggering and decreasing `B(M1)`?
- How robust are pure-M1 and imported-branching assumptions?
