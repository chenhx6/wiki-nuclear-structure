# `103Rh`

## Identity

`Z=45, N=58` odd-mass nucleus. Suzuki 2008 measures lifetimes in one member of a proposed positive-parity doublet; Kuti 2014 identifies that positive pair plus two negative-parity candidate pairs and interprets the latter as same-configuration MχD.

## Production Reactions

- `11B(96Zr,4n)103Rh` at `330 MeV`: [[atlas-gammasphere-rdds-103rh-104rh-zr96-330mev]].
- `96Zr(11B,4n)103Rh` at `40 MeV`: [[lbnl-gammasphere-103rh-b11-40mev]].

## Established Level Structure

Suzuki 2008 imports a proposed positive-parity doublet whose separation remains below about `400 keV` and whose `S(I)` is relatively constant. Kuti 2014 establishes four mutually linked negative-parity Bands 3–6, grouped as Bands 3/4 (about `300-keV` same-spin separation) and Bands 6/5 (about `100 keV`). These are candidate fingerprints, not direct geometry measurements.

## Bands

- [[103rh-chiral-doublet-candidate]]: positive-parity `πg9/2^-1⊗ν(h11/2)^2` candidate. Suzuki's lifetime member is called Band 3, but its exact crosswalk to Kuti Bands 1/2 is unresolved.
- [[103rh-negative-parity-yrast-chiral-doublet]]: Kuti Bands 3/4, the lower negative-parity candidate pair.
- [[103rh-negative-parity-excited-chiral-doublet]]: Kuti Bands 6/5, the higher candidate pair assigned the same effective configuration.

## Lifetimes and Transition Strengths

Candidate Band-3 lifetimes are `0.99(27), 0.95(17), 1.0(1), 0.72(23) ps` for `23/2+` through `29/2+`. Table III gives absolute values at `25/2+–29/2+`: `B(M1)=2.3(4), 1.8(2), 1.2(4) μN²` and `B(E2)=0.077(14), 0.14(3), 0.11(4) e²b²`.

## Competing Interpretations

The declining `B(M1)` can be associated with evolving angular-momentum coupling, but shape evolution remains possible. Partner-band lifetimes are absent, so the electromagnetic-similarity criterion is not tested.

For the negative-parity four-band manifold, the same-configuration and aplanar interpretations depend on CDFT/TAC-CDFT/PRM. The PRM overpredicts the excited-pair energies by about `200 keV` and misses its sharp alignment increase; absolute strengths across all four bands are unavailable.

Peng and Chen 2022 adds a different, isotope-systematics calculation for the one-`νh11/2` family: PC-PK1 3D-TAC CDFT predicts planar-to-aplanar onset at `ℏωcrit=0.58 MeV` and broadly reproduces `I(ω)`, alignment, `J(1)` and `B(M1)/B(E2)`. It still cannot calculate the partner band or splitting and must not be used to validate all Kuti configurations.

## Open Questions

- Do the unmeasured partner and higher-spin states show matching or convergent absolute strengths?
- Are the imported configuration and branching assignments stable under later spectroscopy?
- Does a model reproduce both energy splitting and matrix elements without assuming chirality?
- Can the Suzuki and Kuti positive-parity band numbers be crosswalked directly through the earlier level schemes?
- Do partner-resolved lifetimes and a beyond-frozen-core model sustain the same-configuration identity of Bands 3–6?
