# `165Lu`

## Identity

Odd-proton `Z=71`, `N=94` nucleus in the A≈160 TSD region.

## Production Reactions

`139La(30Si,4n)165Lu` at 152 MeV.

## Bands

[[schonwasser-2003-wobbling-165lu]] reports TSD1/TSD2/TSD3 and assigns them `n_w=0/1/2`. TSD2 has DCO-derived large-E2 links and relative strengths; TSD3 is less directly constrained and relies more on homologous `163Lu` systematics.

## Competing Interpretations

TSD2 has a stronger electromagnetic case than TSD3; configuration alternatives and model-dependent homology remain relevant.
