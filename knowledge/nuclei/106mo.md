# `106Mo`

## Identity

`Z=42, N=64` even-even neutron-rich nucleus. Musangu 2021 extends its collective and negative-parity schemes and reanalyzes the proposed soft chiral-vibrational pair.

## Production Reactions

- Prompt product of `252Cf` spontaneous fission: [[lbnl-gammasphere-cf252-fission-mo104-106]].

## Established Level Structure

Fig.2 and Table II preserve the ground-state, one-phonon γ and two-phonon γ structures while extending several negative-parity bands. A new `3-` level is added to Band 5; Bands 6 and 8 gain revised even-spin structures and linking transitions.

## Bands

- [[106mo-bands-4-5-soft-chiral-vibration-candidate]]: negative-parity pair with experimental same-spin separations of about `100-140 keV`.

## Deformation and Collective Features

Prior γ-band evidence motivates softness/triaxiality. The present TPSM calculation adopts `ε=0.24`, `γ=36°`; this remains a model input.

## Competing Interpretations

The common-configuration chiral-vibration reading depends on TPSM. Several predicted connecting-band intensities are above experimental upper limits, and no lifetime-separated absolute strengths or nucleus-specific angular-momentum-component plot are supplied.

## Open Questions

- Can lifetime measurements test the predicted strong collective interband E2 matrix elements?
- Does a shape-dynamical calculation improve on fixed-deformation TPSM at high spin?
- How robust is the revised Band-5 `12-` crosswalk to earlier analyses?
