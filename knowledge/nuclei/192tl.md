# `192Tl`

## Identity

`Z=81, N=111` odd-odd nucleus. Guo 2022 uses constrained RMF to predict two favorable triaxial minima, not two observed chiral doublets.

## Model Structure

States D*/H* have `(β,γ)=(0.21,39.84°)` and `(0.16,26.17°)` with unpaired `πh9/2⊗νi13/2^-1`. Their full occupations and deformations differ, so the proposed MχD mechanism is not the same-identical-configuration case used for `103Rh`.

## Evidence Boundary

The paper imports one known rotational band and compares its energies/ratios with neighboring Tl isotopes, but identifies no experimental partner in `192Tl`. The doublet count is a model prediction from minima.
